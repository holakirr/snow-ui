Image frames a picture, a logo or an icon as the kit's rounded square: a cover, a product photo, a workspace logo, a choice in an image picker. It is the Figma "Image": 12 to 80px or any size (`free`), with the Hover and Selected states and the Option mark.

```tsx
import { Image } from '@holakirr/snow-ui'

<Image size={64}>
  <img src="/covers/hills.jpg" alt="Hills under a lilac sky" />
</Image>
```

<Primary />

## When to use

- A picture or a logo shown as a rounded square: a thumbnail in a list, a product, a workspace.
- An image picker: covers, themes, templates, with the selection mark (`option`).

## When not to use

- A person: use [Avatar](?path=/docs/components-avatar-avatar--docs), which is round and shows initials until the photo loads.
- An icon at the Figma icon sizes, with a tile and a badge: use [IconBox](?path=/docs/components-iconbox--docs).
- A large content image in an article: use your own `<img>` or framework image.

## Anatomy

1. **Frame**: a `<span>` of `size`, on a Black/4% placeholder, with rounded corners (about 30% of the size; the kit's squircle where the browser supports `corner-shape`). It crops its content to its corners.
2. **Content** (the children): your `<img>`, `<picture>`, framework image or icon, stretched to the frame (`object-fit: cover`). With `icon` it sits inset on the tile.
3. **Option mark** (`option`, optional): the kit's RadioAlt on the top end corner, `aria-hidden`.

| 80 | 24 | 12 | 20, 8 from the corner |
| `free` | 16 | 12 | 20, 8 from the corner |

With `free`, set the size with a class: `className="h-24 w-40"`.

## Variants

`icon` (Figma `Icon=True`) insets the content on the Black/4% tile, with smaller corners: for a logo or an icon rather than a photo.

## States

<Canvas of={ImageStories.States} />

- **Hover** (`interactive`, Figma `State=Hover`): the Figma "Image hover" inner shadow over the top edge on hover, and a pointer cursor. The shadow's transition stops for reduced motion.
- **Selected** (`selected`, Figma `State=Selected`): the option mark is checked; without `option`, a 2px Primary ring shows it. `data-state="selected"` is set for your own styles.
- **Option** (`option`, Figma `Option=True`): the mark, unchecked (a Background/3 disc with the `control-border` ring, `control-border-strong` on hover) or checked (a Primary ring about 30% of its diameter).

## Accessibility

- The frame has no role: give the `<img>` its `alt` (empty when the image is decorative, as in a picker whose control is named).
- **Selection:** the frame is not a control. For an image picker, make each Image the label of a control that is announced and operable — a visually hidden native radio (as in the Picker story: arrow keys move the choice), a checkbox for several — and name it; the mark and the ring only show what the control says (WCAG 4.1.2, 1.4.1). Show the keyboard focus on the label (`has-focus-visible:outline-*`).
- **Contrast:** the unchecked mark's ring is the `control-border` token: the Figma Black/20% by default, WCAG AA (3:1) with more contrast. The checked mark and the selected ring are Primary (black; indigo in dark mode).

<Canvas of={ImageStories.Picker} />

None of the package's deviations from the Figma kit apply to Image.

## Localization and RTL

- Image renders no built-in strings.
- The option mark is on the top end corner: top right, top left in right-to-left text.

## Do and don't

<DoDont>
<Do>

- Give every meaningful image an `alt`.
- Put picker images in real controls (a native radio or checkbox) with names.
- Use one size per list or grid.

</Do>
<Dont>

- Don't use Image for people: use Avatar.
- Don't rely on the mark alone to say what is selected.
- Don't stretch a photo with `free` without a class that keeps its proportions.

</Dont>
</DoDont>
