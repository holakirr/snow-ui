`IconBox` takes `glass`: the Figma `Glass` tile, White/20% with the "Glass 1" effect (the `glass-1` utility: a background blur and a soft shadow) instead of Black/4%, for icons over a picture or a colour. It implies `background`, with the same padding, radius and badge position, except at 80, where the Figma Glass tile is 88px (padding 4, radius 24) where the Black/4% one is 104px; `data-glass` is set on the box.
