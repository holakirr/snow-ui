Chip is a short coloured label: the status of an order, the type of a record, a category. It is the Figma "Chip": seven colours, tinted (`background`) or a coloured dot and text, in two sizes.

```tsx
import { Chip } from '@holakirr/snow-ui'

<Chip color="green">Complete</Chip>
<Chip color="orange" background={false}>Approved</Chip>
```

<Primary />

## When to use

- A status or a category next to other data: a table cell, a list row, a card header.
- A few values that users scan by colour, always with their name.

## When not to use

- A removable filter or a selectable option: use [Tag](?path=/docs/components-tag--docs) (`onRemove`, `state="active"`).
- A count on an icon or an avatar: use [Badge](?path=/docs/components-badge--docs).
- A message about the page: use an [Alert](?path=/docs/components-alert--docs).

## Anatomy

1. **Container**: a `<span>` (or the `asChild` element), `inline-flex`, as wide as its text.
2. **Dot** (without `background`): the Figma Dot icon in the Secondary colour, `aria-hidden`: a 4.5px dot in a 12px box, or, `big`, a 7px ring in a 16px box.
3. **Label** (the children): 12/16 Regular, or 14/20 when `big`.

## Variants

| Prop | Figma | Look |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `background` (default) | Background=True | a tint of the colour: H 20, padding 4/2, radius 4 (`big`: a pill, H 28, padding 12/4, radius 80) |
| `background={false}` | Background=False | a dot and coloured text: H 16, no padding (`big`: H 20) |
| `big` | Big=True | 14/20 text, a 16px ring for the dot |

| `color` | Dot (Figma) | Text | Tint |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `purple` (default) | Secondary/Purple | Purple mixed with 45% `black` | Purple at 10% |
| `indigo` | Secondary/Indigo | Indigo mixed with 45% `black` | Indigo at 10% |
| `blue` | Secondary/Blue | Blue mixed with 45% `black` | Blue at 10% |
| `green` | Secondary/Green | Green mixed with 45% `black` | Green at 10% |
| `orange` | Secondary/Orange | Orange mixed with 45% `black` | Orange at 10% |
| `red` | Secondary/Red | Red mixed with 45% `black` | Red at 10% |
| `grey` | Black/40% | `text-secondary` | Black/4% |

The `black` token is white in dark mode, so the text gets lighter there. The dot, the text and the tint are the `--chip-dot`, `--chip-color` and `--chip-fill` custom properties: `className="[--chip-color:var(--color-black)]"` changes the text only.

## States

Chip has no states of its own: it is a label, not a control. Put interactive behaviour on the element you render with `asChild` (a link to the filtered list) and give it its own hover style.

## Accessibility

- Chip has no role: its text is the information, read in the flow of the table cell or row it sits in. The dot is `aria-hidden`.
- The colour is never the only cue: always show the status name (WCAG 1.4.1).
- **Contrast:** the Figma text is the Secondary colour itself, 1.5–2.4:1 on white. The text here mixes it with 45% `black` (white in dark mode): at least 4.63:1 on the tints over `background-2` and 5.13:1 on white in light mode, 5.66:1 in dark mode (1.4.3). Grey text is `text-secondary` (5.27:1 on its Black/4% tint). The same colours apply with more contrast; the tints and the dots stay the Figma ones, since the text carries the meaning.
- A chip rendered as a link or a button with `asChild` is 20px high (16 without `background`, 28 when `big`): give a small one `relative hit-area` for a 24px pointer target (2.5.8), as the AsChild story does.

Deviations from the Figma kit (the full table is in the package README): the text colours above.

## Localization and RTL

- Chip renders no built-in strings: translate the label.
- The dot is on the start side: on the right in right-to-left text.

## Composition (asChild)

`asChild` puts the chip's classes, `data-color` and props on its only child, with the dot before the child's own children. The child's classes win conflicts.

## Do and don't

<DoDont>
<Do>

- Keep the label short: one or two words.
- Use the same colour for the same meaning everywhere in the app.
- Use the dot style in dense tables and the tinted style where a chip needs more weight.

</Do>
<Dont>

- Don't rely on the colour alone: the label must say the status.
- Don't make a chip interactive without a hover style and a 24px target.
- Don't use chips for long text: they don't wrap.

</Dont>
</DoDont>
