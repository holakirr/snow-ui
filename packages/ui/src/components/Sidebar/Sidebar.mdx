Sidebar is the app's side navigation: a column of grouped menu items that collapses off screen or to icons, and becomes a sheet on small screens. It is composed of parts inside a `SidebarProvider`, which holds the open state. The look is the Figma dashboard sidebar: 212px wide, no fill (it sits on the page background), a 0.5px Black/10% line on its inner edge, 14px section headings and 36px items with radius 12 and a Black/4% fill on hover and on the active item.

```tsx
import {
  Sidebar,
  SidebarContent,
  SidebarGroup,
  SidebarGroupContent,
  SidebarGroupLabel,
  SidebarInset,
  SidebarMenu,
  SidebarMenuButton,
  SidebarMenuItem,
  SidebarProvider,
  SidebarTrigger,
} from '@holakirr/snow-ui'

<SidebarProvider>
  <Sidebar collapsible="icon">
    <SidebarContent>
      <SidebarGroup>
        <SidebarGroupLabel>Dashboards</SidebarGroupLabel>
        <SidebarGroupContent>
          <SidebarMenu>
            <SidebarMenuItem>
              <SidebarMenuButton asChild isActive tooltip="Overview">
                <a href="/overview" aria-current="page">
                  <StarIcon />
                  <span>Overview</span>
                </a>
              </SidebarMenuButton>
            </SidebarMenuItem>
          </SidebarMenu>
        </SidebarGroupContent>
      </SidebarGroup>
    </SidebarContent>
  </Sidebar>
  <SidebarInset>
    <SidebarTrigger />
    {/* the page */}
  </SidebarInset>
</SidebarProvider>
```

The sidebar is fixed to the edge of the viewport, so the stories below with a collapsible sidebar render in their own frame. Press the trigger (or Ctrl+B / ⌘B inside the frame) to collapse it.

## When to use

- The main navigation of an app or a dashboard, with more destinations than fit in a header.
- Navigation grouped into sections, with nested pages.

## When not to use

- A panel of details or a form next to the page: [Sheet](?path=/docs/components-sheet--docs).
- A few top-level sections: [Tabs](?path=/docs/components-tabs--docs) or links in a header.
- A single list of items inside the page: [ListItem](?path=/docs/components-listitem--docs) or [IconText](?path=/docs/components-icontext--docs) (`interactive`).
- The path to the current page: [Breadcrumb](?path=/docs/components-breadcrumb--docs).

## Anatomy

1. **Provider** (`SidebarProvider`): holds the state and renders a flex `<div>` at least the viewport's height. It sets `--sidebar-width` (212px) and `--sidebar-width-icon` (52px), which its `style` can override, and a `TooltipProvider` without delay. `useSidebar()` reads the state inside it.
2. **Sidebar** (`Sidebar`): the panel. `side`, `variant` and `collapsible` set its place, look and collapsed form.
3. **Header and footer** (`SidebarHeader`, `SidebarFooter`): columns at the top and bottom, padding 8 / 16px, 8px gap.
4. **Content** (`SidebarContent`): the middle, which scrolls; groups are 8px apart.
5. **Group** (`SidebarGroup`): a section, padding 8 / 16px, with:
   - `SidebarGroupLabel`: the heading, 28px high, 12px side padding, radius 12, 14px Regular in `black-80`;
   - `SidebarGroupAction` (optional): a 20px button at the heading's end, with a 24px hit area (36px below 768px);
   - `SidebarGroupContent`: the section's menu, 14px text.
6. **Menu** (`SidebarMenu`, a `<ul>` with 4px gaps, and `SidebarMenuItem`, an `<li>`), with:
   - `SidebarMenuButton`: the Figma nav item ("Frame"), a `<button>` or, with `asChild`, your link. Padding 8px, radius 12, an 8px gap, 14px text and 20px icons. `isActive`, `variant`, `size` and `tooltip` (see below);
   - `SidebarMenuAction` (optional): a 20px button at the item's end, with a 24px hit area (36px below 768px); `showOnHover` hides it on desktop until the item is hovered or focused;
   - `SidebarMenuBadge` (optional): a 20px-high count at the item's end, 12px tabular figures;
   - `SidebarMenuSkeleton`: a 36px loading row, with a 20px icon placeholder when `showIcon`.
7. **Submenu** (`SidebarMenuSub`, `SidebarMenuSubItem`, `SidebarMenuSubButton`): a nested list with a 1px Black/10% line on its start side. Sub buttons are `<a>`s, 36px (`size="md"`, the default) or 28px (`sm`) high, with 16px icons and `isActive`.
8. **Separator** (`SidebarSeparator`): a Separator with 16px side margins.
9. **Input** (`SidebarInput`): an Input, 32px high and full width.
10. **Trigger** (`SidebarTrigger`): a Button with the sidebar icon that opens and closes the sidebar.
11. **Rail** (`SidebarRail`, optional): a 16px strip on the sidebar's inner edge that toggles it on click and shows a 2px Black/10% line on hover.
12. **Inset** (`SidebarInset`): the `<main>` next to the sidebar, a sibling after `Sidebar`, with a `background-1` fill.

### Collapsing

| `collapsible` | Collapsed | Small screens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `offcanvas` (default) | slides off the edge of the screen, then is hidden (`visibility: hidden`): out of the tab order and the accessibility tree | a sheet |
| `icon` | a 52px column of icons (68px for `floating` and `inset`): group labels, group and item actions, badges and submenus hide, menu buttons become 36px squares | a sheet |
| `none` | never: a static 212px column | the same column (no sheet) |

### Menu button

| Prop | Values | Look |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `size` | `default` (36px, 14px text), `sm` (28px, 12px text), `lg` (48px, 14px text) | height and text |
| `variant` | `default`, `outline` | `outline` adds a `background-1` fill and a 0.5px Black/10% stroke |
| `isActive` | `boolean` | a Black/4% fill (`data-active`) |
| `tooltip` | a string, or `TooltipContent` props | a tooltip with the label, shown only while the sidebar is collapsed on desktop |

## States

- **Expanded and collapsed:** the sidebar element has `data-state="expanded"` or `"collapsed"`, and `data-collapsible` set to the `collapsible` value while collapsed; style your own parts with `group-data-[collapsible=icon]:…`.
- **Hover, pressed and active** (`isActive`): a Black/4% fill on menu and sub buttons.
- **Keyboard focus:** the `focus-ring` utility on buttons, links, labels and actions.
- **Disabled:** `disabled` or `aria-disabled` on a menu or sub button: 50% opacity and no pointer events.
- **Loading:** `SidebarMenuSkeleton` rows in place of the menu items. The text placeholder gets a random width between 50% and 90%.

## Accessibility

- The desktop sidebar is a set of `<div>`s with no landmark: wrap its navigation in a `<nav>` with an `aria-label`. `SidebarInset` is a `<main>`.
- Menus are `<ul>` lists. `SidebarMenuButton`, `SidebarGroupAction` and `SidebarMenuAction` render a `<button>` without a `type`, so inside a form pass `type="button"`. With `asChild` the child (a link) is the control.
- `isActive` only sets `data-active` for the fill: put `aria-current="page"` on the current page's link yourself.
- `SidebarTrigger` is named "Toggle Sidebar" (visually hidden text, `messages.sidebar.toggle`); `aria-label` replaces it. It sets `aria-expanded` (the sidebar is open; on small screens, the sheet) and `aria-controls`: the `id` of each `Sidebar` of the provider (yours, or a generated one; several sidebars get different ids).
- `SidebarRail` is for pointers only (`tabIndex={-1}`), named and titled "Toggle Sidebar"; the trigger is the keyboard path.
- Collapsed to icons, the menu buttons keep their text in the DOM (clipped), so their names stay; `tooltip` shows the label on hover and keyboard focus.
- On small screens the sheet is a modal Radix Dialog: focus moves into it, a click outside closes it, and its title "Sidebar" and description "Displays the mobile sidebar." are for screen readers only. It has no close button.
- `showOnHover` actions become visible when their item has keyboard focus.

### Keyboard

Every button, link and field is a tab stop; there are no arrow keys between items:

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Tab / Shift + Tab | move to the next / previous button, link or field, in DOM order. `SidebarRail` is not a tab stop. A collapsed `offcanvas` sidebar is `visibility: hidden` once it has slid out, so Tab skips it (and screen readers don't find it). In the small-screen sheet, focus stays inside the sheet |
| Enter, Space | activate the focused button (`SidebarTrigger` opens and closes the sidebar); Enter follows a link |
| Ctrl + B / ⌘ + B | open or close the sidebar (the sheet on small screens) from anywhere on the page: `SidebarProvider` listens for a lowercase `b` on `window` and prevents the browser's default. Not in text fields and rich-text editors, where it stays their shortcut (bold), nor after a handler has called `preventDefault()` |
| Escape | close the small-screen sheet |

### Deviations from the Figma kit

The full table is in the package README.

| Figma | Library | WCAG |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Focus" effect: a 4px Black/4% ring (1.1:1) | `focus-ring`: the Figma ring plus a 2px `black-80` outline offset by 2px (12.6:1 light, 8.7:1 dark) | 2.4.7, 1.4.11 |
| Section headings (`SidebarGroupLabel`) in Black/40%: 2.85:1 at 14px | `black-80` (12.6:1 light, 8.7:1 dark) | 1.4.3 |

## Localization and RTL

- Built-in strings come from `messages.sidebar`: `toggle` (`SidebarTrigger` and `SidebarRail`), `title` and `description` (the small-screen sheet). `aria-label` on `SidebarTrigger` overrides `toggle`.
- `side="start"` (the default) and `"end"` follow the `dir` of `SnowUIProvider`: in right-to-left text a `start` sidebar is on the right. The rendered element gets the physical side as `data-side`; `left` and `right` stay physical.
- In right-to-left text the edge line moves to the sidebar's left, the trigger icon is mirrored, actions and badges sit at the left end of items, the submenu line on the right, and tooltips open to the left.

## Composition (asChild)

`SidebarGroupLabel`, `SidebarGroupAction`, `SidebarMenuButton`, `SidebarMenuAction` and `SidebarMenuSubButton` take `asChild`: the only child (a router link, or another component's trigger) is rendered with the part's classes, the child's winning conflicts.

```tsx
<SidebarMenuButton asChild isActive={pathname === '/projects'}>
  <RouterLink to="/projects" aria-current={pathname === '/projects' ? 'page' : undefined}>
    <StarIcon />
    <span>Projects</span>
  </RouterLink>
</SidebarMenuButton>

{/* A menu button that opens a DropdownMenu. */}
<DropdownMenuTrigger asChild>
  <SidebarMenuButton>Username</SidebarMenuButton>
</DropdownMenuTrigger>
```

`SidebarTrigger` and `SidebarRail` run your `onClick` first; `event.preventDefault()` in it skips the toggle.

The desktop state is `open` (controlled) or `defaultOpen` (uncontrolled, `true` by default), with `onOpenChange(open)`, on `SidebarProvider`; without `open`, the sidebar toggles itself and `onOpenChange` only reports it. Every change is saved in the `sidebar_state` cookie (`SIDEBAR_COOKIE_NAME`) for 7 days, and in the pre-5.1 `sidebar:state` cookie too, for servers that read that one by name (until 6.0: move them to `readSidebarState`, which reads both). In the browser the saved state wins over `defaultOpen`, after hydration, so server-rendered markup always hydrates without a mismatch. It is read once, when the provider mounts: a cookie saved later by another tab or another provider doesn't move this sidebar. To render the saved state on the server too (no flash of the expanded sidebar), read the cookie there with `readSidebarState`, which isn't a client function, and pass it as `defaultOpen`:

```tsx
// Next.js App Router: app/layout.tsx (a Server Component)
import { headers } from 'next/headers'
import { readSidebarState, SidebarProvider } from '@holakirr/snow-ui'

export default async function Layout({ children }: { children: ReactNode }) {
  const cookie = (await headers()).get('cookie')
  return (
    <SidebarProvider defaultOpen={readSidebarState(cookie) ?? true}>
      {children}
    </SidebarProvider>
  )
}
```

The small-screen sheet's state isn't a prop: use `useSidebar()`, which returns `state`, `open`, `setOpen`, `openMobile`, `setOpenMobile`, `isMobile` and `toggleSidebar` (and throws outside a `SidebarProvider`).

## Do and don't

<DoDont>
<Do>

- Keep a visible `SidebarTrigger`: the rail is for pointers and the shortcut for keyboards.
- Mark the current page with `isActive` and `aria-current="page"` on its link.
- Give menu buttons a `tooltip` when the sidebar collapses to icons.
- Wrap the navigation in a `<nav>` with an `aria-label`, and the page in `SidebarInset`.
- Put the label of a menu button in a `<span>` last, so it truncates.

</Do>
<Dont>

- Don't call `useSidebar` outside a `SidebarProvider`: it throws.
- Don't render two providers on one page: each one toggles on Ctrl+B / ⌘B.
- Don't put menu buttons inside a form without `type="button"`.
- Don't hide the only way to a destination in a submenu or a `showOnHover` action: they disappear when the sidebar collapses to icons.

</Dont>
</DoDont>

## Props

The parts render plain elements, or library components (`SidebarTrigger` a Button, `SidebarInput` an Input, `SidebarSeparator` a Separator), with the Figma styles: each takes the props below, `className` (merged with the part's classes) and the attributes of the element it renders. The parts with `asChild` render the only child instead. `useSidebar()` returns the state inside a `SidebarProvider` (see Composition).

### SidebarProvider

Holds the state and renders the layout's flex `<div>`. The desktop state is `open` with `onOpenChange` (controlled) or `defaultOpen` (uncontrolled, `true` by default), saved in the `sidebar_state` cookie (and the pre-5.1 `sidebar:state` one, which is still read) and read once, when it mounts. It handles Ctrl + B / ⌘ + B.
