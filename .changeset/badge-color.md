`Badge` and `BadgeComponent` get `color`: `"indigo"` (the default, unchanged) or `"red"`, the kit's red badge. The red dot is Secondary/Red; the red number is the per-mode `white` on `red-text` (5.21:1, 8.65:1 in dark mode) instead of the kit's white on Secondary/Red (3.36:1). New type: `BadgeColor`.
