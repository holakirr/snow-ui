New `Chip`, the Figma "Chip": a short coloured label, such as a status in a table cell. Seven colours (`color`: `purple`, `indigo`, `blue`, `green`, `orange`, `red`, `grey`), tinted (`background`, the default: H 20, padding 4/2, radius 4; `big`, a pill: H 28, padding 12/4, radius 80) or a coloured dot and text (`background={false}`), in 12/16 or, `big`, 14/20. The text mixes the Secondary colour with 45% of `black` (white in dark mode), so it reads at 4.5:1 or more where the Figma colours are 1.5–2.4:1; the dot and the tint keep the kit's colours. `asChild` puts the chip's look on a link or a button.
