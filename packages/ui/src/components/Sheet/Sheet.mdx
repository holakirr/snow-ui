Sheet is a modal panel that slides in from an edge of the screen: details, filters, settings, or navigation on small screens. It is the same Radix Dialog as [Dialog](?path=/docs/components-dialog--docs), laid out as a side panel: a glass panel over the Dialog mask, with a built-in close button.

```tsx
import {
  Button,
  Sheet,
  SheetContent,
  SheetDescription,
  SheetHeader,
  SheetTitle,
  SheetTrigger,
} from '@holakirr/snow-ui'

<Sheet>
  <SheetTrigger asChild>
    <Button variant="outline" label="Filters" />
  </SheetTrigger>
  <SheetContent>
    <SheetHeader>
      <SheetTitle>Filters</SheetTitle>
      <SheetDescription>Narrow down the list.</SheetDescription>
    </SheetHeader>
    {/* content */}
  </SheetContent>
</Sheet>
```

<Primary />

## When to use

- Details or settings for something on the page, in a panel from the side.
- Filters or a secondary form that can be longer than a dialog.
- Navigation on small screens: the mobile [Sidebar](?path=/docs/components-sidebar--docs) is a sheet.

## When not to use

- A short task or a confirmation that needs the user's full attention: [Dialog](?path=/docs/components-dialog--docs).
- A few controls anchored to a button, without blocking the page: [Popover](?path=/docs/components-popover--docs).
- A list of actions: [DropdownMenu](?path=/docs/components-dropdownmenu--docs).
- A permanent side panel that stays open next to the content: [Sidebar](?path=/docs/components-sidebar--docs).

## Anatomy

1. **Root** (`Sheet`): holds the open state. It renders nothing itself.
2. **Trigger** (`SheetTrigger`, optional): the element that opens the sheet, usually a Button (`asChild`).
3. **Mask** (`SheetOverlay`, rendered by `SheetContent`): the Dialog mask, a gradient from #CBDDFF at 50% to #D7D0FF at 20% with a 20px background blur.
4. **Panel** (`SheetContent`): the `role="dialog"` element, in a portal, on the `side` edge. `background-3` (Figma Background/3) with a 20px background blur, 16px padding and a 0.5px `black-10` border on its inner edge.
5. **Close button** (built into `SheetContent`): 16px from the top and end edges; a Borderless `sm` icon button with a 24px close icon.
6. **Header** (`SheetHeader`): the title and the description, 8px apart; centred on small screens, start-aligned from the `sm` breakpoint.
7. **Title** (`SheetTitle`): 18/28 semibold, `black`. It names the sheet.
8. **Description** (`SheetDescription`, optional): 14/20 `text-secondary`. It describes the sheet.
9. **Footer** (`SheetFooter`, optional): the actions. On small screens a column with the last child on top; from `sm` a row aligned to the end, 8px apart.
10. **Close** (`SheetClose`, optional): closes the sheet from your own button.

`SheetPortal` and `SheetOverlay` are exported for custom compositions; `SheetContent` already renders both.

## Sides

<Canvas of={SheetStories.Side} />

| `side` | Edge | Size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `end` (default) | right in left-to-right text, left in right-to-left text | full height; 75% of the width, at most 384px from `sm` |
| `start` | left in left-to-right text, right in right-to-left text | as `end` |
| `left`, `right` | that edge, in any direction | as `end` |
| `top`, `bottom` | that edge | full width; as tall as its content |

The panel's `data-side` attribute holds the physical edge (`left`, `right`, `top` or `bottom`). The side panels are `w-3/4` with `sm:max-w-sm`: to make one wider, replace the cap too, e.g. `<SheetContent className="sm:max-w-[540px]">`.

## States

- **Open and closed:** `open` / `defaultOpen` / `onOpenChange` on `Sheet`. The panel slides in from its edge and out to it; the mask fades.
- **Close button:** the Borderless button states: Black/4% on hover, scaled to 95% when pressed, the `focus-ring` on keyboard focus.

Open, to compare the mask and the glass panel in both themes: [Open story](?path=/story/components-sheet--open) ([dark](?path=/story/components-sheet--open-dark)).

## Portal and theme

`SheetContent` renders its mask and panel in a portal at the end of `<body>`, outside your layout. So it takes the theme of `<html>`, not of a `data-theme` scope around the sheet. Set the theme on `<html>`, or give the sheet its own scope: `<SheetContent data-theme="dark">` (the prop is passed through). The mask keeps the same colours in both themes.

## Accessibility

- `SheetContent` is a `role="dialog"` element. `SheetTitle` names it and `SheetDescription` describes it. Always render a `SheetTitle`; hide it with `className="sr-only"` if the design has no visible title, as the mobile Sidebar does.
- The sheet is modal (Radix `modal`, on by default):
  - Opening moves focus into the panel, to its first focusable element: the close button.
  - The rest of the page is hidden from assistive technology (`aria-hidden`) and ignores the pointer; the mask locks the page scroll.
- Escape, the close button, a `SheetClose` or a click on the mask closes it. Focus then returns to the trigger.
- The close button's accessible name is "Close" (`messages.sheet.close`); `closeLabel` replaces it. It is the only built-in string.

### Keyboard

The sheet's own keys (Radix Dialog); the fields and buttons inside keep theirs:

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Enter, Space | on the trigger: open the sheet and move focus to the close button; on the close button or a `SheetClose`: close it |
| Tab / Shift + Tab | move to the next / previous focusable element in the panel; after the last one focus wraps to the first (and back), so it doesn't leave the sheet |
| Escape | close the sheet and return focus to the trigger; a menu, Select or Popover open inside the sheet takes Escape first |

## Localization and RTL

- `messages.sheet.close` ("Close") names the close button; `closeLabel` on `SheetContent` overrides it.
- `side="start"` and `side="end"` resolve with the direction of `SnowUIProvider`: `end` is the right edge in left-to-right text and the left edge in right-to-left text. Without a provider (or a Radix `DirectionProvider`) they resolve as left-to-right, whatever `<html dir>` says. `left` and `right` stay physical.
- The portalled panel takes `dir` from the provider. The close button sits at the top end corner (top left in right-to-left text), and the header aligns to the start.

## Composition (asChild)

`SheetTrigger` and `SheetClose` take `asChild` (Radix) to render your own button. Control the sheet with `open` and `onOpenChange`:

```tsx
const [open, setOpen] = useState(false)

<Sheet open={open} onOpenChange={setOpen}>
  <SheetTrigger asChild>
    <Button variant="outline" label="Edit profile" />
  </SheetTrigger>
  <SheetContent side="start">
    <SheetHeader>
      <SheetTitle>Edit profile</SheetTitle>
    </SheetHeader>
    {/* fields */}
    <SheetFooter>
      <SheetClose asChild>
        <Button variant="gray" label="Cancel" />
      </SheetClose>
      <Button variant="filled" label="Save" onClick={() => save().then(() => setOpen(false))} />
    </SheetFooter>
  </SheetContent>
</Sheet>
```

## Do and don't

<DoDont>
<Do>

- Always render a `SheetTitle`, visible or `sr-only`.
- Use `start` and `end` for side panels, so they follow the text direction.
- Keep the sheet about one thing on the page, with its actions in `SheetFooter`.
- Set `data-theme` on `SheetContent` when it needs a theme other than the one on `<html>`.

</Do>
<Dont>

- Don't add your own close button in the top corner: `SheetContent` has one.
- Don't use a sheet for a short confirmation: use a Dialog.
- Don't widen a side panel with a `w-*` class alone: `sm:max-w-sm` still caps it at 384px.

</Dont>
</DoDont>

## Props

`Sheet`, `SheetTrigger`, `SheetPortal`, `SheetOverlay`, `SheetContent`, `SheetTitle`, `SheetDescription` and `SheetClose` are the [Radix Dialog](https://www.radix-ui.com/primitives/docs/components/dialog#api-reference) parts with the Figma styles; `SheetHeader` and `SheetFooter` are this library's `<div>`s. They take the props below, `className` (merged with the part's classes) and the attributes of the element they render.

### Sheet

The root: it holds the open state and renders nothing. Controlled with `open` and `onOpenChange`, or uncontrolled with `defaultOpen`; `modal` is `true` by default.

<ArgTypes of={Sheet} />

### SheetTrigger

A `<button>` with `aria-haspopup="dialog"` and `aria-expanded` that opens the sheet; with `asChild` your element (usually a Button) is the trigger.

<ArgTypes of={SheetTrigger} />

### SheetPortal

Renders its children in a portal, in `container` (`<body>` by default). `SheetContent` already renders one; use it for a custom composition.

<ArgTypes of={SheetPortal} />

### SheetOverlay

The mask, a fixed `<div>` over the page. `SheetContent` already renders one; use it inside a `SheetPortal` for a custom composition.

<ArgTypes of={SheetOverlay} />

### SheetContent

The `role="dialog"` panel, in a portal with the mask, on the `side` edge, with the close button first (named by `closeLabel`). `onOpenAutoFocus` and `onCloseAutoFocus` can prevent the focus moves; `onEscapeKeyDown` and `onInteractOutside` can keep the sheet open.

<ArgTypes of={SheetContent} />

### SheetHeader

A `<div>` for the title and the description.

<ArgTypes of={SheetHeader} />

### SheetFooter

A `<div>` for the actions: a column on small screens, a row from `sm`.

<ArgTypes of={SheetFooter} />

### SheetTitle

An `<h2>` that names the sheet (`aria-labelledby`).

<ArgTypes of={SheetTitle} />

### SheetDescription

A `<p>` that describes the sheet (`aria-describedby`).

<ArgTypes of={SheetDescription} />

### SheetClose

A `<button>` that closes the sheet; with `asChild` your element (e.g. a Cancel Button) is the close button.

<ArgTypes of={SheetClose} />
