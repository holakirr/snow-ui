Strip draws a row or a column of equal segments, 8px apart. It is the Figma "Strip" (`Count`, `Vertical`): 2px thick and Black/100% by default. With `value` it becomes a segmented progress bar: that many segments stay Black/100% and the rest are Black/10%.

```tsx
import { Strip } from '@holakirr/snow-ui'

<Strip count={7} value={4} thickness={4} rounded aria-label="Storage used" />
```

<Primary />

## When to use

- Progress in steps: storage used, onboarding steps, a quota.
- A strength meter, e.g. for a password (`role="meter"`).
- Decorative bars, such as the simple bar chart of the Figma examples.

## When not to use

- A line between content: use [Separator](?path=/docs/components-separator--docs).
- A value people set: use [Slider](?path=/docs/components-input-slider--docs).
- An exact percentage: Strip fills whole segments only.

## Anatomy

1. **Track:** a `<div>`, 160px wide in a row or 134px high in a column by default, with `data-orientation`. Set another length with `className` (`w-full`) or `style`.
2. **Segments:** `count` equal `<span>`s, 8px apart, with `data-state="filled"` or `"empty"`. `thickness` sets their height (their width in a column); `rounded` rounds their ends.

## States

Without `value` every segment is filled. With `value`, the first `value` segments are filled (Black/100%) and the rest empty (Black/10%):

<Canvas of={StripStories.Progress} />

`count` is rounded down and is at least 1; `value` is clamped between 0 and `count`. Pass a whole number: a fraction is rounded up on screen (2.5 fills 3 segments) but not in `aria-valuenow`.

Segments have `data-state`, so you can recolour them: `[&>[data-state=filled]]:bg-…`, or `[&>:first-child]:bg-indigo` for the first one. The examples from the Figma Strip page (a quota, a password strength meter and a bar chart):

## Accessibility

- Without `value`, Strip is decorative: `aria-hidden="true"`.
- With `value`, it is a `progressbar` with `aria-valuemin` 0, `aria-valuemax` = `count` and `aria-valuenow` = the filled segments. Give it a name with `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`.
- It isn't focusable and is not a tab stop, with or without `value`.
- For a measurement rather than progress (password strength, disk usage), pass `role="meter"`, and `aria-valuetext` when the number means little on its own ("Weak").
- Filled segments are Black/100% against Black/10% empty ones. If you recolour them, keep the filled ones distinguishable, and show the value in text too, as the Figma examples do ("86 of 100 Used").

None of the package's deviations from the Figma kit apply to Strip.

## Localization and RTL

- A row follows the text direction: in right-to-left text the segments fill from the right.
- Strip renders no built-in strings; translate `aria-label` and `aria-valuetext`.

## Do and don't

<DoDont>
<Do>

- Name every Strip that has a `value`.
- Use `role="meter"` and `aria-valuetext` for a strength or a level.
- Show the value in text next to the strip.
- Use `thickness` and `rounded` for the Figma examples' heavier bars.

</Do>
<Dont>

- Don't use Strip as a divider.
- Don't pass a fraction as `value`.
- Don't rely on a custom colour alone to show which segments are filled.

</Dont>
</DoDont>

## Props

Strip renders a `<div>` of `<span>` segments and takes the `<div>` attributes (`role`, `aria-label`, `aria-valuetext`…), but no `children`; `className` merges with its classes.
