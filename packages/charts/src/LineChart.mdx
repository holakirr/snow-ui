A line chart shows how one or more series change over ordered categories (days, months). It is the SnowUI dashboards' "Revenue" block: 1px smooth lines, the comparison period dashed, 12px axis labels, and a tooltip on hover and keyboard focus.

```tsx
import { LineChart } from '@holakirr/snow-ui-charts'

<LineChart
  title="Revenue"
  data={data} // [{ month: 'Jan', current: 8000, previous: 6000 }, …]
  xKey="month"
  config={{
    current: { label: 'Current week', color: 'primary' },
    previous: { label: 'Previous week', color: 'cyan', dashed: true },
  }}
/>
```

## When to use

- Trends over time or another ordered axis, for one to about eight series.
- Comparing a period with the previous one (a `dashed` series), or actuals with a forecast (`projectionFrom`).

## When not to use

- Parts of a whole: [DonutChart](?path=/docs/charts-donutchart--docs).
- Amounts per unordered category: [BarChart](?path=/docs/charts-barchart--docs).
- Volume under the line matters (totals, stacked shares): [AreaChart](?path=/docs/charts-areachart--docs).
- A trend next to a KPI number, without axes: [Sparkline](?path=/docs/charts-sparkline--docs).

## Anatomy

1. **Legend** (with two series or more; `legend` forces it on or off): each series' dot and label, and optionally a total after it (`legendValues`, Semibold, like the Figma "Current Week  $58,211"). A dashed series has a hollow dot.
2. **Value axis**: compact numbers (`0`, `10K`, `20K`, `30K`), 4 ticks in steps of 1, 2 or 5 × 10ⁿ (`tickCount`, `domain`, `yTickFormatter`).
3. **Gridlines and baseline** (`grid`): 0.5px Black/4% lines and a 0.5px Black/20% baseline. The Figma dashboards hide them (`grid={false}`).
4. **Lines**: 1px (`strokeWidth`), monotone curves (`curve`: `smooth`, `linear`, `step`); `dots` marks every point.
5. **Category axis**: the `xKey` values (`xTickFormatter`); labels that would overlap are skipped.
6. **Tooltip**: on hover and keyboard focus, with a Black/20% cursor line and a 12px dot on each line.

## Variants

**Dashed series and projections.** `dashed: true` in a series' config dashes it (`2 4`, round caps). `projectionFrom="Apr"` draws every other series solid up to April and dashed after it; April's value appears once in the tooltip.

<Canvas of={LineChartStories.Projection} />

**Fade.** `fade` makes each line fade in from 40% opacity at its start to 100% at its end, like the Figma lines (it follows the direction in right-to-left charts).

**Many series** take the palette in order; `tooltip="light"` uses the glass tooltip.

## Accessibility

- The chart is a named figure with a visually hidden table of every value (see [ChartContainer](?path=/docs/charts-chartcontainer--docs#accessibility)). Name it with `title` or `aria-labelledby` (the card heading), and summarise it in `description`.
- Tab focuses the chart and shows the first point; ← / → move between points and the values are announced.
- Tell series apart by more than colour: labels in the legend, `dashed` for the comparison series.

## Do and don't

<DoDont>
<Do>

- Keep the current period Primary and the comparison Cyan and dashed, as in Figma.
- Keep series under about eight; split or filter beyond that.
- Start the value axis at 0 unless the change is the point (`domain`).

</Do>
<Dont>

- Don't connect unordered categories with a line: use bars.
- Don't rely on `fade` for meaning: it only softens the start of the line.

</Dont>
</DoDont>
