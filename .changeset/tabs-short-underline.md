`TabsList` gets `underline`: `"full"` (the default, unchanged) or `"short"`, the kit's short underline from the Line docs — a rounded Primary dash, 6×3px, centred under the active `line` tab's label, 6px below it. The tabs get 3px taller with it. The segmented variants ignore it. New type: `TabsUnderline`.
