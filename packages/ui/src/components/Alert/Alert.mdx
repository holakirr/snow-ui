Alert (a callout) is a message in the page flow: what happened, or what the user should know, next to the content it is about. It is a tinted card with a status icon, a title, a description, optional actions and an optional dismiss button, in five statuses: `default` (neutral), `info`, `success`, `warning` and `error`.

```tsx
import { Alert, AlertDescription, AlertTitle, Button } from '@holakirr/snow-ui'

<Alert status="error" action={<Button variant="outline" label="Retry" />}>
  <AlertTitle>Payment failed</AlertTitle>
  <AlertDescription>Your card was declined.</AlertDescription>
</Alert>
```

## When to use

- The result of an action that the user needs to see where they are: a form that didn't submit, a payment that failed, settings that were saved.
- A condition of the page or the account: storage almost full, a trial ending, a maintenance window.
- A note or a tip in the content (`default` or `info`).

## When not to use

- A short confirmation that can disappear on its own ("Saved"): a toast ([Toaster](?path=/docs/components-toaster--docs)).
- A question the user must answer before going on ("Delete this project?"): [AlertDialog](?path=/docs/components-alertdialog--docs).
- A form field's error: `FormMessage` next to the field ([Form](?path=/docs/components-form--docs)).
- Loading: [Spinner](?path=/docs/components-spinner--docs) or [Progress](?path=/docs/components-progress--docs).

## Anatomy

1. **Container** (`Alert`): the Figma "Card" shape, radius 16 and padding 12/16, filled with the status's tint. A `<div>` (or the `asChild` element) with the status's live-region role.
2. **Status label**: visually hidden text naming the status ("Error"), read before the title.
3. **Icon** (`icon`, optional): a 20px status icon on the title's line, 12px before the text. `null` hides it; any node replaces it.
4. **Title** (`AlertTitle`): 14/20 Semibold, black.
5. **Description** (`AlertDescription`): 14/20 Regular, `text-secondary`, 4px under the title.
6. **Actions** (`action`, optional): after the text, 16px from it, or under it (12px) when the alert is too narrow for both (the text keeps at least 256px).
7. **Dismiss button** (`onDismiss`, optional): a 24×24px Borderless button with a 16px close icon, at the end.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `default` (default) | Black/4% (the kit's neutral fill) | Info, black | `status` |
| `info` | Secondary/Blue at 16% | Info, blue mixed with 40% black | `status` |
| `success` | Secondary/Green at 16% | CheckCircle, green mixed with 40% black | `status` |
| `warning` | Secondary/Yellow at 16% | Warning, yellow mixed with 40% black | `alert` |
| `error` | Secondary/Red at 16% | WarningCircle, red mixed with 40% black | `alert` |

The fill and the icon colour are the `--alert-fill` and `--alert-icon` custom properties, so a class can change them: `className="[--alert-fill:var(--color-color-2)]"`. In dark mode the icon mixes the colour with white instead (the `black` token flips), so it stays readable in both themes. The icons are the "fill" weight of Phosphor's Info, CheckCircle, Warning and WarningCircle, the kit's icon set.

## States

Alert has no states of its own: it is shown or not. The dismiss button has the Borderless button states (Black/4% on hover, 95% scale when pressed, the `focus-ring` on keyboard focus). Dismissing is yours: `onDismiss` is called, and you remove the alert.

## Accessibility

- **Role:** `warning` and `error` are `role="alert"` (assertive: announced at once, interrupting), the other statuses `role="status"` (polite: announced when the screen reader is idle). Pass `role` to change it, e.g. `role="note"` or a named `role="region"` for a static callout that is part of the page and shouldn't be announced.
- **Announcements:** screen readers announce an `alert` when it is added to the page. A `status` is announced reliably only when its content changes while it is already in the page: to announce a success message, render the `Alert` up front and fill it in, or use a toast.
- **Status:** the status is read before the content, as visually hidden text ("Error", then "Payment failed…"), so it doesn't depend on the colour or the icon (WCAG 1.4.1). `statusLabel` changes it; an empty string turns it off. `default` has none.
- **Icon:** `aria-hidden`; the status label says what it shows.
- **Dismiss button:** a native button named "Dismiss" (`dismissLabel`). When it removes the alert, the focus is lost with it: move it somewhere useful in `onDismiss` (the Dismissible story focuses the section heading).
- **Contrast:** the title is black and the description `text-secondary` on every tint: at least 5.2:1 in both modes (1.4.3). The icons are at least 3.6:1 on the light tints and 5:1 on the dark ones (1.4.11); the Figma Secondary colours alone would be 1.5–3.4:1 on white.

## Localization and RTL

- `messages.alert.dismiss` ("Dismiss") names the dismiss button, and `messages.alert.info`, `success`, `warning` and `error` ("Information", "Success", "Warning", "Error") are the status labels; `dismissLabel` and `statusLabel` override them.
- The layout uses flex and logical margins: in right-to-left text the icon is on the right and the dismiss button on the left. The status icons aren't mirrored.
- Right-to-left: [RTL story](?path=/story/components-alert--rtl).

## Composition (asChild)

`Alert`, `AlertTitle` and `AlertDescription` take `asChild`. On `Alert` the child (a `<section>`, an `<aside>`) gets the classes, the role and the props, and the icon, the actions and the dismiss button go around its own children; the child's `role` wins. On `AlertTitle` it makes the title a heading, on `AlertDescription` a paragraph.

```tsx
<Alert asChild status="info" role="region">
  <section aria-labelledby="release-notes">
    <AlertTitle asChild>
      <h2 id="release-notes">Release notes</h2>
    </AlertTitle>
    <AlertDescription asChild>
      <p>Version 5.1 adds Alert, AlertDialog, Progress and Spinner.</p>
    </AlertDescription>
  </section>
</Alert>
```

## Do and don't

<DoDont>
<Do>

- Say what happened and what to do next: "Your card was declined. Use another card."
- Put the alert next to what it is about: above the form, at the top of the section.
- Keep one action, or two at most.
- Move the focus after a dismiss.

</Do>
<Dont>

- Don't use `warning` or `error` for information: they interrupt screen-reader users.
- Don't stack several alerts: merge them, or show the most important one.
- Don't use an alert for something that needs an answer before going on: use an AlertDialog.
- Don't colour the text with the status colours: the title and the description stay black and `text-secondary`.

</Dont>
</DoDont>
