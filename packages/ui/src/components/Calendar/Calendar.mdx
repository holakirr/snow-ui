Calendar shows a month grid to pick a date, several dates or a range. It is [react-day-picker](https://daypicker.dev) (v10) styled as the Figma DatePicker: a glass surface (Background/3, "Glass 2", a 1px Surface/1 stroke, radius 16), weeks starting on Monday, 12px days, the selected day in Primary and today in Secondary/Indigo. The library adds the Figma toolbar ("Today", "Last selection", the month and its arrows), an optional header row and a year view.

```tsx
import { Calendar } from '@holakirr/snow-ui'

const [date, setDate] = useState<Date | undefined>()

<Calendar mode="single" selected={date} onSelect={setDate} />
```

<Primary />

## When to use

- Picking a date, several dates or a date range where seeing the weekdays helps: bookings, reports, deadlines.
- A date picker in a [Popover](?path=/docs/components-popover--docs), with a date field in `header`.

## When not to use

- A date the user knows by heart, such as a birth date: an [Input](?path=/docs/components-input-input--docs) is faster to type.
- Events at times of day across a week: [Scheduler](?path=/docs/components-scheduler--docs).
- Choosing from a few preset periods ("Last 7 days"): a [Select](?path=/docs/components-input-select--docs) or a [ToggleGroup](?path=/docs/components-input-togglegroup--docs).

## Anatomy

1. **Surface** (the `Root` slot): the glass container, radius 16. `className` goes here.
2. **Header** (`header`, optional): your content above the toolbar, e.g. a date field. Padding 16px, a 0.5px Black/10% line below.
3. **Toolbar** (the month caption, 28px high, 12px text), one per month:
   - the actions (`showTodayButton`, `lastSelection`, first month only): 20px-high Black/4% chips, radius 8, 4px side padding, with a 24px pointer target (`hit-area`, WCAG 2.5.8);
   - the previous and next buttons: Button Small "Borderless" icon buttons with 16px arrows, in a `nav` landmark;
   - the caption label: the month name. With `showYearSwitcher` (the default) it is a button that opens the year view; `captionLayout="dropdown"` (react-day-picker) shows month and year dropdowns instead.
4. **Weekdays**: a 38px row of 12px weekday names in `text-secondary`.
5. **Days** (the month grid, 16px below the toolbar, 328px wide): seven columns of 38px-high day buttons, radius 12, 12px text.
6. **Footer** (`footer`, react-day-picker, optional): 12px `black-80` text below the months.

The months have 16px of padding and are 16px apart.

### Year view

With `showYearSwitcher`, the caption label opens a view of `yearRange` years (12 by default) in four columns: the page that holds the year shown, where pages are counted from five years before the current year. Each year is a 40px button with radius 12; the current year has a Black/4% fill, and years wholly outside `startMonth` / `endMonth` are disabled. The previous and next buttons page through the years ("Go to the previous 12 years"). Picking a year shows it in the days view, in the month of the selection (or the current month), and returns the focus to the caption label. With several months, the year view shows one caption; after a year is picked the months come back and the first month's caption label has the focus.

## States

- **Hover:** a Black/4% fill on a day.
- **Keyboard focus:** the `focus-ring` utility; the focused day is raised above its neighbours.
- **Selected:** a Primary fill with the per-mode `white` text (white on black, black on the dark-mode indigo), Primary hover on hover. With forced colours, the system Highlight.
- **Range:** the start, middle and end days form one Primary band; the start and end keep their rounded outer corners.
- **Today:** a Secondary/Indigo fill with static black text (Indigo at 80% on hover), unless the day is selected. In the light theme that is all, as in the kit. In dark mode, where a selected day is indigo too, today has no fill: indigo text and a 4px dot under the number, so it stays apart from a selected day, and a selected today (indigo, with the dot) from an unselected one. The dot also marks a selected today in the light theme and today with forced colours, in the system text colour.
- **Outside days:** the previous and next month's days in `text-secondary`. They are real dates and can be selected.
- **Disabled** (`disabled`, react-day-picker matchers): Black/20% text, no hover fill.

## Accessibility

- react-day-picker renders each month as a `grid` named by the month ("January 2025"), with `gridcell`s that carry `aria-selected` and, with a `mode` (or `onDayClick`), a day button named by its full date. In `multiple` and `range` mode the grid is `aria-multiselectable`.
- The previous and next buttons are named "Go to the previous month" and "Go to the next month" and sit in a `navigation` landmark named "Month navigation". With several months there is still one landmark. They are disabled at `startMonth` / `endMonth`.
- The toolbar actions and the year switcher are native buttons. The year switcher is a disclosure: it has `aria-expanded`, and `aria-controls` pointing to the year view while that is open. The year view is a `grid` named by its years ("2021 - 2032"): rows of four `gridcell`s, each with a native button named by its year. The year shown in the days view is `aria-selected`, the current year `aria-current="date"` (not only its Black/4% fill).

### Keyboard

The days of all the months shown share one tab stop (react-day-picker v10):

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Tab / Shift + Tab | move through the header, the toolbar buttons (the actions, previous, the year switcher, next) and the days. The days' tab stop is the last focused day, else the selected day, else today, else the first enabled day. The years share one tab stop too: the last focused year, else the year shown, else the current year, else the first enabled year |
| ArrowRight / ArrowLeft | move focus to the next / previous day; in right-to-left text ArrowLeft moves to the next day |
| ArrowDown / ArrowUp | move focus to the same day of the next / previous week |
| Shift + ArrowRight / Shift + ArrowLeft | move focus a month forward / back (the other way round in right-to-left text) |
| Shift + ArrowDown / Shift + ArrowUp | move focus a year forward / back |
| PageDown / PageUp | move focus a month forward / back; with Shift, a year |
| Home / End | move focus to the first / last day of the week (`weekStartsOn`) |
| Enter, Space | select the focused day (with a `mode`) and call `onDayClick`; on a toolbar button, activate it; on the year switcher, open or close the year view |
| Enter, Space (on a year) | show that year in the days view and return the focus to the year switcher |

In the year view:

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| ArrowRight / ArrowLeft | move focus to the next / previous year; in right-to-left text ArrowLeft moves to the next year |
| ArrowDown / ArrowUp | move focus four years forward / back (the year below / above) |
| Home / End | move focus to the first / last year of the row |
| Ctrl + Home / Ctrl + End | move focus to the first / last year shown |
| PageDown / PageUp | show the next / previous years (as the next / previous buttons) and move focus as far |

The moves skip disabled days, turn the month when focus leaves the months shown, and stop at `startMonth` / `endMonth`. In the year view the moves show the next or previous years when focus leaves the years shown, and stop at the years outside `startMonth` / `endMonth`; Tab reaches the years after the next button, and Shift+Tab goes back through the next button, the switcher and the previous button. Alt and Shift combinations, and Ctrl or ⌘ with keys other than Home and End, are left to the browser. Opening or closing the year view keeps the focus on the switcher, also with several months, where the year view shows one. Escape isn't handled by the year view: in a [Popover](?path=/docs/components-popover--docs) it closes the popover.

## Localization and RTL

- `SnowUIProvider`'s `locale` (a date-fns locale, or a `react-day-picker/locale` one that also translates the day picker's own labels) names the months, weekdays and days; the `locale` prop wins over the provider. It sets the first day of the week only with `SnowUIProvider`'s `weekStartsOn="locale"` (`locale.options.weekStartsOn`: Sunday for `enUS`, the default; Monday for `ru`); `weekStartsOn` wins over it.
- Built-in strings come from `messages.calendar`: `today` and `lastSelection` (the actions), `previousMonth`, `nextMonth` and `navigation` (the arrows and their landmark), and `previousYears(count)` / `nextYears(count)` (the arrows in the year view). The English defaults of `previousMonth`, `nextMonth` and `navigation` give way to a react-day-picker locale's own labels; a translated message wins over them.
- `todayLabel` and `lastSelectionLabel` override the action labels, and react-day-picker's `labels` overrides the navigation labels.
- `SnowUIProvider`'s `dir` (or the `dir` prop) mirrors the calendar: the actions are on the right, the arrows point the other way, a range's rounded ends swap sides, and ArrowLeft moves to the next day.

## Controlled and uncontrolled

Calendar keeps react-day-picker's names: the selection is `selected` / `onSelect` (with `mode`), the shown month `month` / `defaultMonth` / `onMonthChange`. The year view and its page are internal state.

## Do and don't

<DoDont>
<Do>

- Set `mode` and handle `onSelect`: without a `mode` (or `onDayClick`) the days are not buttons.
- Bound the dates with `startMonth` / `endMonth`, and disable dates that can't be picked with `disabled`.
- Put a date field in `header` when users may type the date.
- Use a `react-day-picker/locale` locale in `SnowUIProvider` to translate the day picker's own labels too.

</Do>
<Dont>

- Don't use Calendar for a date far in the past, such as a birth date: a text field is faster.
- Don't show outside days with several months: they repeat the neighbouring month's dates.
- Don't override the `components` slots the library uses (`MonthCaption`, `CaptionLabel`, `MonthGrid`, `Root`, `Chevron`) unless you replace the toolbar and the year view too.

</Dont>
</DoDont>

## Props

Calendar is react-day-picker's `DayPicker` with the Figma styles, toolbar and year view. `className` goes to the surface (the `Root` slot), merged with its classes; of the other attributes, the surface gets `style`, `id`, `title`, `lang`, `role`, `aria-label`, `aria-labelledby` and `data-*`.

### Calendar

The table shows the library's own props and the react-day-picker props used most (`mode`, `selected`, `onSelect`, `month`, `disabled`…). Calendar takes every other [react-day-picker prop](https://daypicker.dev/api/type-aliases/DayPickerProps) too, such as `formatters`, `labels`, `classNames`, `components`, `hideNavigation` and `onPrevClick`. The `…ClassName` props add classes to one part each, merged with its defaults (`captionClassName` is deprecated: use `monthCaptionClassName`).

### react-day-picker props Calendar changes

| Prop | Here |
| --- | --- |
| `weekStartsOn` | `1` (Monday) by default, or `SnowUIProvider`'s `weekStartsOn` (`"locale"`: the locale's first day); a value wins over the provider |
| `showOutsideDays` | `true` for one month, `false` for several |
| `locale`, `dir` | taken from `SnowUIProvider` when not set |
| `labels` | merged over the navigation labels from `messages.calendar` |
| `hideNavigation` | hides the toolbar's previous / next buttons; react-day-picker's own navigation is always replaced by the toolbar |
| `onPrevClick`, `onNextClick` | called by the toolbar buttons, in the year view too |
| `today` | also the date the "Today" action goes to |
| `classNames`, `components` | merged over the library's, slot by slot |
