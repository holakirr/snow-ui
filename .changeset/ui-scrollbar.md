New `scrollbar-snow` utility (in `theme.css`, and in `index.css`): the Figma "Scrollbar" on your scroll containers, a 4px rounded thumb that widens to 8px under the pointer, in the `control-border` colour (the Figma Black/20%, 3:1 or more with more contrast), with no track. Chrome, Edge and Safari draw the kit's thumb; Firefox its thin scrollbar in the same colour. Nothing animates, and forced-colors mode keeps the system scrollbar. See Foundations › Scrollbar.
