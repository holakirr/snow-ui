DateRangePicker is a form field for a range of dates (and times, with `withTime`): it shows the picked range and opens the Figma DatePicker in range mode to pick another. The top area shows the start and the end ("10 / 22 / 2026 – 10 / 29 / 2026"), the one being picked in black and the other dimmed. The first pick is the start, the second the end, and the calendar closes. Everything else — the field, the top area, the views, the limits, the keyboard, localization and forms — works as in [DatePicker](?path=/docs/components-datepicker--docs).

```tsx
import { type DateRange, DateRangePicker, Label } from '@holakirr/snow-ui'

const [stay, setStay] = useState<DateRange | null>(null)

<Label htmlFor="stay">Stay</Label>
<DateRangePicker id="stay" value={stay} onValueChange={setStay} numberOfMonths={2} />
```

## When to use

- A period in a form: a stay, a sprint, a report's dates.

## When not to use

- One date: [DatePicker](?path=/docs/components-datepicker--docs).
- A few preset periods ("Last 7 days", "This month"): a [Select](?path=/docs/components-input-select--docs) or a [ToggleGroup](?path=/docs/components-input-togglegroup--docs), with a DateRangePicker for "Custom" if needed.

## Anatomy

As in [DatePicker](?path=/docs/components-datepicker--docs#anatomy). The field writes a range within one month with the month and the year once, as the kit's formats do: "Jan 13 – 16, 2025", with an en dash, the range sign, where the kit's format sheet seems to use a hyphen ("13–16 янв. 2025 г." in Russian: the order and the dash of `Intl.DateTimeFormat#formatRangeToParts` in the `locale`, the month of the "PP" dates). Any other range shows two full dates in the `dateFormat` default "PP", "Jan 13, 2025 – Feb 2, 2025", as does a range without an end ("Jan 13, 2025 – …"): `messages.datePicker.range(start, end)`. So does a locale whose range format repeats the month or writes it in digits (Japanese, Chinese, Korean). A one-day range shows that day. A `dateFormat`, `withTime` or a `messages.datePicker.range` of your own always shows two dates, in the `dateFormat` and joined by that message. With no range the field shows the placeholder "Pick a date range". The top area has the start date, "–" and the end date (the one not being picked in `text-secondary`), then the time of the one being picked with `withTime`. `numberOfMonths` shows several months side by side (one by default, so the popover fits a phone).

## Picking a range

- Every time the calendar opens, a new range starts: the first click (or Enter) picks the start, the second the end, and the calendar closes. If the end comes before the start, the two are swapped. The same day twice is a one-day range.
- The date being picked is the active one in the top area, and it switches by itself: the start, then the end. A click on the other date in the top area picks that one instead, keeping the date that is already there (a new end for the same start, say). Typing an end before the start, or a start after the end, turns the date red and isn't taken.
- While the end is being picked, the range follows the pointer.
- Closing the calendar after the first pick (Escape, a click outside) keeps the range the field had.
- `onValueChange` is called once, with the complete range `{ from, to }`, or with `null` when the field is cleared. Picking the range the field already has calls nothing.
- With a controlled `open` that stays `true` (an "Apply" button closes it, say), each range still starts anew after the previous one's end.
- **`excludeDisabled`:** a range can't cover a day that can't be picked (`minDate`, `maxDate`, `disabledDates`): an end past one starts a new range there, and the preview stops before it. Use it for bookings, where a taken night splits the calendar.

## Date range and time

`withTime` (and `withSeconds`, `hourCycle`) is the Figma "Date range and time" type: the time in the top area is the one of the date being picked, the start's while the start is active, the end's while the end is. A picked end keeps the popover open to set its time; Enter in the top area or a click outside closes it with the range.

## Accessibility

- As in [DatePicker](?path=/docs/components-datepicker--docs#accessibility): a `<button role="combobox">` that opens a modal `dialog`, here named "Choose a date range", with focus on the range's start (or today).
- The calendar is a `grid` with `aria-multiselectable`; the days of the range are `aria-selected`.
- The top area has two groups of spinbuttons, "Start date" and "End date"; the one being picked has `aria-current`. Focusing a date (or clicking it) makes it the one being picked; the time group is "Start time" or "End time" to match.
- `name` adds a hidden input with the range as an ISO 8601 interval: `2025-01-13/2025-01-16` (see [Native forms](#native-forms)).

### Keyboard

As in [DatePicker](?path=/docs/components-datepicker--docs#keyboard), except that Enter picks a start, then an end (the Default story's test and the unit tests):

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Enter, Space, ArrowDown | on the field: open the calendar, with focus on the range's start (or today) |
| Backspace, Delete | on the field: clear the range (`clearable`) |
| Arrow keys, Page Up / Page Down, Home / End | in the calendar: move between days, weeks and months |
| Enter, Space | in the calendar: pick the start, then the end; the second pick closes the calendar and focus returns to the field |
| 0–9, arrow keys, Backspace, Enter | in the top area: as in DatePicker, for the start and the end dates and the time |
| Escape | close the calendar; after only a start, the field keeps its previous range |

## Localization and RTL

- As in DatePicker: `SnowUIProvider`'s `locale` formats the dates and localizes the calendar, `dateFormat` sets the format, the week starts on Monday unless you pass `weekStartsOn` or set `SnowUIProvider`'s `weekStartsOn`.
- `messages.datePicker` holds `rangePlaceholder` ("Pick a date range"), `rangeDialog` ("Choose a date range"), `range(start, end)` ("start – end", for two full dates; your own also joins a range within one month: see [Anatomy](#anatomy)) and `clear` ("Clear date"); the top area's `startDate`, `endDate`, `startTime` and `endTime`, and the rest as in DatePicker.
- In right-to-left text the range reads from the right.

## Forms

Bind `value` and `onValueChange` (a `DateRange`, or `null`); pass `field.ref`, `field.onBlur`, `field.name` and `field.disabled` as they are. With zod: `z.object({ from: z.date(), to: z.date() }, { error: 'Pick your dates.' })`.

### Native forms

| | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Submits | `name=yyyy-MM-dd/yyyy-MM-dd`, an ISO 8601 interval (e.g. `2025-01-13/2025-01-16`; with `withTime` `2025-01-13T09:30/2025-01-16T18:00`), or `name=` (empty) with no range or a range without an end |
| `required` | the form doesn't submit until both ends are set |
| `form="id"`, reset, `disabled` | as in [DatePicker](?path=/docs/components-datepicker--docs#native-forms): a form reset drops a half-picked range and brings back the range the field had on mount (a controlled field gets `onValueChange` with it) |

A controlled `value` without an end (`{ from }`) shows as "Jan 20, 2025 – …" (`messages.datePicker.range(start, '…')`) and isn't submitted. An `Invalid Date` start counts as no range.

## Controlled and uncontrolled

Pass `defaultValue` to leave the range to the picker, or `value` with `onValueChange(range)` to control it; `null` is no range. `DateRange` is react-day-picker's `{ from, to? }`, re-exported. The popover: `defaultOpen`, or `open` with `onOpenChange`.

## Composition

As in DatePicker: no `asChild`; `className` and `style` go to the field, the button props to the trigger, `contentClassName` to the popover, `calendarProps` to the Calendar.

## Do and don't

<DoDont>
<Do>

- Show two months (`numberOfMonths={2}`) where there is room, so ranges across months are easy to pick.
- Set `minDate` and `maxDate` when only some dates make sense.

</Do>
<Dont>

- Don't use two DatePickers for a start and an end date: a range picker shows the range and keeps the ends in order.
- Don't pass `mode`, `selected` or `onSelect` in `calendarProps`: the picker owns the selection.

</Dont>
</DoDont>
