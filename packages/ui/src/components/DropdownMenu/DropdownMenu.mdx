DropdownMenu opens a list of actions or options from a button: an account menu, a "more" menu, view options. The menu is the Figma "Popover" surface with 36px items, check marks, group labels, separators, shortcuts and submenus. It is built on Radix Dropdown Menu.

```tsx
import {
  Button,
  DropdownMenu,
  DropdownMenuContent,
  DropdownMenuItem,
  DropdownMenuShortcut,
  DropdownMenuTrigger,
} from '@holakirr/snow-ui'

<DropdownMenu>
  <DropdownMenuTrigger asChild>
    <Button variant="outline" label="Account" />
  </DropdownMenuTrigger>
  <DropdownMenuContent align="start" className="w-60">
    <DropdownMenuItem onSelect={openProfile}>
      Profile
      <DropdownMenuShortcut keys={['⌘', 'P']} separator="" />
    </DropdownMenuItem>
    <DropdownMenuItem onSelect={logOut}>Log out</DropdownMenuItem>
  </DropdownMenuContent>
</DropdownMenu>
```

## When to use

- A set of actions behind one button, when there isn't room for a button each.
- Options that change a view: a checkbox item for a setting, a radio group for one of a few choices.
- Nested actions, in a submenu.

## When not to use

- Picking a value for a form field: [Select](?path=/docs/components-input-select--docs).
- Actions on right-click: [ContextMenu](?path=/docs/components-contextmenu--docs) (with the same actions reachable elsewhere).
- Navigation between pages: links, e.g. [Sidebar](?path=/docs/components-sidebar--docs) or [Tabs](?path=/docs/components-tabs--docs).
- Content that isn't a list of actions (fields, text): [Popover](?path=/docs/components-popover--docs).
- Searching many commands: [CommandPalette](?path=/docs/components-commandpalette--docs).

## Anatomy

1. **Root** (`DropdownMenu`): holds the open state. It renders nothing itself.
2. **Trigger** (`DropdownMenuTrigger`): the element that opens the menu, usually a Button (`asChild`).
3. **Content** (`DropdownMenuContent`): the `role="menu"` panel, in a portal, 4px from the trigger (`sideOffset={4}`), at least 128px wide. The Figma Popover surface: 12px padding, a 16px radius, `background-3`, a 1px `surface-1` stroke and Glass 2. Taller than the room on its side of the trigger, it stops at the edge of the window and scrolls, with the kit's scrollbar (`scrollbar-snow`, in an 8px gutter at the end while it scrolls); submenus too. Portal submenus (`DropdownMenuPortal`): the content clips what overflows it.
4. **Item** (`DropdownMenuItem`): 36px high (8px padding), a 12px radius, 14/20 text, 16px icons 8px apart. `variant="destructive"` (added in 5.2) makes it the kit's red row: text and icons in `red-text` (see [Destructive item](#destructive-item)). `inset` adds 32px of start padding, e.g. to line up items without an icon with items that have one.
5. **Checkbox item** (`DropdownMenuCheckboxItem`) and **radio item** (`DropdownMenuRadioItem`, in a `DropdownMenuRadioGroup`): a 16px check mark in a slot 8px from the end, like Select's (the Figma selected item). Both kinds show the check; the role (`menuitemcheckbox`, `menuitemradio`) tells them apart.
   **Switch item** (`DropdownMenuSwitchItem`, added in 5.2): a checkbox item that ends in the kit's Switch (28×16) instead of the check, as the kit's "Wrap Column" row. See [Switch item](#switch-item).
6. **Label** (`DropdownMenuLabel`, optional): a group title, 14/20 `text-secondary`, 28px high (the kit's SearchPopup group title); takes `inset`.
7. **Group** (`DropdownMenuGroup`, optional): groups items, with a 4px margin above and below (it merges into a separator's 7 and 8px).
8. **Separator** (`DropdownMenuSeparator`): a 1px `black-4` line, 7px under the item above and 8px over the item below: 16px between the groups' items, as the kit's item group (8px padding above and below, a 1px Black/4% stroke inside its bottom edge).
9. **Shortcut** (`DropdownMenuShortcut`, optional): a `KBD` (12/16) at the end of an item, from `keys`, as the kit's plain text in `text-secondary` (Figma: "⌘C" in Black/40%, no key cap). `separator=""` joins symbol keys as in the kit (`⌘C`, not `⌘+C`); a `variant` (`solid`, `border`) brings back the KBD key cap.
10. **Search** (`search` on `DropdownMenuContent`, optional, added in 5.2): the kit's Search field (Gray, 28px high, 4/8 padding, a 16px radius) in a 44px row with 8px of padding at the top of the menu; the items scroll under it. See [Search](#search).
11. **Submenu** (`DropdownMenuSub`): a `DropdownMenuSubTrigger` (an item with a 16px `text-secondary` chevron at the end, and an optional `hint` with the current value before it) and a `DropdownMenuSubContent` on the same surface. Wrap the sub content in `DropdownMenuPortal`, as the stories do.

The Figma Popover as a menu, open: [Open story](?path=/story/components-dropdownmenu--open).

## States

- **Highlighted** (hover or keyboard focus): Black/4% fill. A sub trigger keeps it while its submenu is open.
- **Checked:** a check mark at the end (`checked` on a checkbox item, or the radio group's `value`); a switch item's switch is on (`primary` track, the thumb at the end).
- **Destructive** (`variant="destructive"`, added in 5.2): `red-text` text and icons, on the same Black/4% highlight; dimmed like any item when disabled.
- **Disabled** (`disabled`): Black/20% text and a default arrow cursor; the arrow keys and typeahead skip it.
- **Open and closed:** `open` / `defaultOpen` / `onOpenChange` on `DropdownMenu`.

## Search

Added in 5.2. `search` on `DropdownMenuContent` puts a search field at the top of the menu that filters its items as you type. The kit's rule: give a menu the search when it has **more than 10 items, or more than 5 and a submenu**. The library doesn't count your items: you decide with `search`.

```tsx
<DropdownMenuContent search className="w-60">
  <DropdownMenuItem>Ask AI</DropdownMenuItem>
  {/* … more than 10 items … */}
</DropdownMenuContent>

// Or with options:
<DropdownMenuContent
  search={{ placeholder: 'Find a property', emptyMessage: 'No property', onQueryChange }}
>
```

<Canvas of={DropdownMenuStories.SearchOpen} />

- **Matching:** an item, checkbox item, radio item or sub trigger shows while its text contains every word of the query, in any case (`defaultDropdownMenuSearchFilter`, the CommandPalette default). The text is `textValue`, or the text of the item's children (icons and a `DropdownMenuShortcut` from `keys` add none). `filter(text, query)` replaces the matching.
- **While filtering**, the matches are one list, as in the kit's "Have results": labels and separators are hidden and groups lose their margins. A submenu isn't filtered: its trigger is.
- **Empty:** "No results" (`emptyMessage`, or `messages.dropdownMenu.empty`) under the field, in a polite live region. The empty menu is hidden.
- **Query:** uncontrolled, it starts from `defaultQuery` every time the menu opens; or controlled with `query` and `onQueryChange`.
- **Focus:** the field takes the focus when the menu opens (from the keyboard too, instead of the first item), so you type straight away. See [Keyboard](#keyboard).

## Switch item

Added in 5.2. `DropdownMenuSwitchItem` is the kit's "Wrap Column" row: a setting that turns on and off, with the kit's Switch at the end of the item (a 28×16 track, `control-border` off and `primary` on, a 12px thumb that moves to the end; a Black/10% or Black/20% track when disabled). It takes the props of a checkbox item, with a boolean `checked` and `onCheckedChange(checked)`.

- **Semantics:** the item is the `menuitemcheckbox`, with `aria-checked`. The switch is a picture of that state (`aria-hidden`, no `role="switch"`): a menu item can't contain a control of its own, so don't put a [Switch](?path=/docs/components-input-switch--docs) in a `DropdownMenuItem`.
- **Selecting** it (a click, Enter, Space) toggles it and closes the menu, as a checkbox item. To toggle in place, call `event.preventDefault()` in `onSelect`, as the story does.
- In right-to-left text the thumb moves to the left when on.

```tsx
<DropdownMenuSwitchItem
  checked={wrap}
  onCheckedChange={setWrap}
  onSelect={(event) => event.preventDefault()}
>
  <TextAlignLeftIcon />
  Wrap column
</DropdownMenuSwitchItem>
```

## Destructive item

Added in 5.2. `variant="destructive"` on `DropdownMenuItem` marks an action that deletes or can't be undone, as the kit's red "Delete Property" row: the text and the icons (which take `currentColor`) are `red-text`, as AlertDialog's red action. `red-text` is #D42020 in light mode; in dark mode it is mixed with 40% white, so it keeps 4.5:1 on the item's highlight too. The role stays `menuitem`: say what the item does in its label ("Delete property"), not with the colour alone.

```tsx
<DropdownMenuItem variant="destructive" onSelect={remove}>
  <TrashIcon />
  Delete property
</DropdownMenuItem>
```

<Canvas of={DropdownMenuStories.Destructive} />

The kit confirms in place: after a click the row reads "Confirm Deletion?" and a second click deletes. Keep the menu open on the first selection with `event.preventDefault()` in `onSelect` and change the label, as the story below does. A screen reader may not announce the focused item's new name, so for an action that can't be undone, confirm in an [AlertDialog](?path=/docs/components-alertdialog--docs) with its `destructive` action instead.

## Portal and theme

`DropdownMenuContent` renders in a portal at the end of `<body>`, outside your layout. So it takes the theme of `<html>`, not of a `data-theme` scope around the trigger. Set the theme on `<html>`, or give the menu its own scope: `<DropdownMenuContent data-theme="dark">` (the prop is passed through; give a portalled sub content the same attribute).

## Accessibility

- The trigger gets `aria-haspopup="menu"` and `aria-expanded`. The content is `role="menu"`; items are `menuitem`, `menuitemcheckbox` (with `aria-checked`) or `menuitemradio`, separators `separator`, groups `group`.
- To keep the menu open after a selection, call `event.preventDefault()` in the item's `onSelect`.
- The menu is modal by default (Radix `modal`): while it is open, focus stays in it, the rest of the page is hidden from assistive technology and ignores the pointer, and the page doesn't scroll. `modal={false}` turns this off.
- When the menu closes, focus goes back to the trigger (not after a click outside a `modal={false}` menu).
- The only built-in strings are the search field's name and empty state (`messages.dropdownMenu`). A `DropdownMenuShortcut` is a `<kbd>` with `aria-keyshortcuts`; it only shows the shortcut, so register the shortcut yourself.
- With `search`, the field (a `searchbox` named "Search", with `aria-controls` on the menu) and the empty state sit next to the `role="menu"` list, in the popover: a menu may only contain items, groups and separators (axe `aria-required-children`). The list keeps the menu's name (the trigger) and `aria-orientation`; the popover itself has no role.

### Keyboard

From the stories' interaction tests and Radix Dropdown Menu; disabled items are skipped:

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Enter, Space, ArrowDown (on the trigger) | open the menu and focus its first item |
| ArrowDown / ArrowUp | move to the next / previous item; no wrapping unless `loop` is set on the content. After a click on the trigger focus is on the menu itself: ArrowDown moves to the first item, ArrowUp to the last |
| Home / End, PageUp / PageDown | move to the first / last item |
| a character | move to the next item whose text starts with the typed characters (typeahead); once typing has started, Space is part of the search |
| Enter, Space | select the item and close the menu (a checkbox item toggles, a radio item is picked); on a sub trigger, open the submenu on its first item |
| ArrowRight (ArrowLeft in right-to-left text) | on a sub trigger: open the submenu and focus its first item |
| ArrowLeft (ArrowRight in right-to-left text) | in a submenu: close it and focus its trigger |
| Escape | close the menu, submenus included, and return focus to the trigger |
| Tab, Shift + Tab | do nothing while the menu is open |

With `search` (added in 5.2), in the field:

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| a character | type in the field and filter the items (no typeahead) |
| ArrowDown / ArrowUp | move to the first / last item shown |
| Escape | clear the field; in an empty field, close the menu |

On an item, a letter goes to the field (the typeahead is off with a search); ArrowUp on the first item goes back to the field (without `loop`). Enter and Space select the item, as without a search.

### Deviations from the Figma kit

The full table is in the package README.

| Figma | Library | WCAG |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Group labels, shortcuts and submenu value hints in Black/40%: 2.85:1 on white, 3.41:1 on #333 | `text-secondary`: Black/60% in light mode (5.74:1 on `background-1`), White/70% in dark mode (7.08:1 on #333, at least 4.76:1 on the lightest popover surface) | 1.4.3 |
| Submenu chevron in Black/20%: 1.6:1 | `text-secondary`, as the Select chevron: Black/60% in light mode (at least 5.5:1, on the highlighted item too), White/70% in dark mode (at least 4.82:1) | 1.4.11 |
| Destructive item (the red "Delete" row; 5.2): red text and icon | `variant="destructive"`: `red-text` (#D42020: 5.21:1 on the popover, 4.77:1 highlighted); in dark mode `red-text` mixed with 40% white (#FFB3B3: 6.21:1, 4.59:1 highlighted), as #FF8080 is 4.36:1 and 3.22:1 there | 1.4.3 |

The kit selects the first result of a search by default ("Have results"). Here the focus stays in the field until ArrowDown moves it to the first result, so a screen reader announces the item before Enter picks it, and Enter in the field doesn't select one you haven't heard.

The Black/4% item highlight (1.1:1) keeps the Figma value with the default contrast: the README lists it as a known gap. With more contrast (`prefers-contrast: more` or `data-contrast="more"`; see [Contrast](?path=/docs/guides-contrast--docs)) the highlighted item also gets a 2px `black-80` ring.

## Localization and RTL

- The menu renders no text of its own (labels and shortcuts are yours to translate), except with `search`: the field's name `messages.dropdownMenu.search` ("Search"), its placeholder `messages.search.placeholder`, and the empty state `messages.dropdownMenu.empty` ("No results"). `label`, `placeholder` and `emptyMessage` in `search` win over the provider.
- The content takes `dir` from `SnowUIProvider`; without a provider (or a Radix `DirectionProvider`) it is `ltr`, whatever `<html dir>` says.
- In right-to-left text the check marks (at the left end), `inset` padding and shortcuts swap sides, the submenu arrow points left, and ArrowLeft opens a submenu (to the left).

## Composition (asChild)

`DropdownMenuTrigger` and the items take `asChild` (Radix): render a Button as the trigger, or a link as an item.

```tsx
<DropdownMenuItem asChild>
  <RouterLink to="/settings">Settings</RouterLink>
</DropdownMenuItem>
```

With `asChild`, calling `event.preventDefault()` in the child's handler skips the menu's own behaviour: an item doesn't close the menu. Checkbox items take `checked` / `onCheckedChange`, a radio group `value` / `onValueChange`, and the root `open` / `onOpenChange`.

## Do and don't

<DoDont>
<Do>

- Start item labels with a verb for actions: "Invite users", "Log out".
- Group related items with `DropdownMenuGroup`, a `DropdownMenuLabel` and separators.
- Wrap `DropdownMenuSubContent` in `DropdownMenuPortal`.
- Show a shortcut only for a shortcut that works.
- Add `search` to a menu with more than 10 items (or more than 5 and a submenu), as the kit does.

</Do>
<Dont>

- Don't put fields or long text in a menu: use a Popover.
- Don't use a menu to pick a form value: use Select.
- Don't nest submenus more than one level deep.
- Don't navigate from an item's `onSelect`: render a link with `asChild`.

</Dont>
</DoDont>

## Props

The parts are the [Radix Dropdown Menu](https://www.radix-ui.com/primitives/docs/components/dropdown-menu#api-reference) parts with the Figma Popover styles; `DropdownMenuShortcut` is a [KBD](?path=/docs/components-text-kbd--docs). They take the props below, `className` (merged with the part's classes) and the attributes of the element they render.

### DropdownMenu

The root: it holds the open state and renders nothing. Controlled with `open` and `onOpenChange`, or uncontrolled with `defaultOpen`; `modal` is `true` by default; `dir` overrides the direction from `SnowUIProvider`.

<ArgTypes of={DropdownMenu} />

### DropdownMenuTrigger

A `<button>` with `aria-haspopup="menu"` and `aria-expanded` that toggles the menu; with `asChild` your element (usually a Button) is the trigger.

<ArgTypes of={DropdownMenuTrigger} />

### DropdownMenuContent

The `role="menu"` panel, in a portal. It is placed below the trigger (`side="bottom"`), centred on it (`align="center"`), 4px away (`sideOffset`, 4 here instead of the Radix 0), and flips when there isn't room. `loop` (off by default) makes the arrow keys wrap around; the `on…` handlers can prevent the menu from closing.

`search` (added in 5.2) adds the search field: `true`, or `{ query, defaultQuery, onQueryChange, filter, label, placeholder, emptyMessage }` (`DropdownMenuSearchOptions`). See [Search](#search).

<ArgTypes of={DropdownMenuContent} />

### DropdownMenuItem

A `menuitem`. `onSelect` runs on a click, Enter or Space; `textValue` sets the typeahead text (and the text a `search` matches) when the item's content isn't plain text; `inset` adds 32px of start padding; `variant="destructive"` (added in 5.2) colours it `red-text` (see [Destructive item](#destructive-item)).

<ArgTypes of={DropdownMenuItem} />

A submenu's root: `open` / `defaultOpen` / `onOpenChange`. It renders nothing.

A `menuitem` with a 16px chevron at the end, in `text-secondary`; it opens the submenu on hover, a click, Enter, Space or ArrowRight (ArrowLeft in right-to-left text). The submenu's `role="menu"` panel, beside its trigger (on the left in right-to-left text), on the same surface. `loop` makes its arrow keys wrap around.
