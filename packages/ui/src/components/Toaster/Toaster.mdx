A toast is a short message about something that just happened: "Saved", "Something went wrong". It shows at the bottom of the screen and closes itself after 3 seconds ("stay 3s" in the Figma guidance), unless it has an action. It is the Figma "Toast" set: a dark glass pill with an optional success or error icon, in two sizes. Render `<Toaster />` once and call `toast()` from anywhere. It is built on Radix Toast.

```tsx
import { Toaster, toast } from '@holakirr/snow-ui'

// Once, e.g. in the root layout.
<Toaster />

// Anywhere, e.g. after a save.
toast({ status: 'success', title: 'Saved' })
```

<Primary />

## When to use

- Confirming an action the user just took: saved, copied, sent.
- A short error that doesn't need an answer, with a way to retry elsewhere.
- An undo for the action just taken (an `action`).

## When not to use

- Something the user must read or decide: a [Dialog](?path=/docs/components-dialog--docs).
- An error about a form field: show it next to the field ([Form](?path=/docs/components-form--docs)).
- Information that must stay on screen: put it in the page.
- A hint about a control: [Tooltip](?path=/docs/components-tooltip--docs).

## Anatomy

1. **Toaster** (`Toaster`): renders the toasts from `toast()` inside a `ToastProvider` and a `ToastViewport`. Props: `duration` (3000ms by default, for toasts without an `action`), `limit` (1 by default: how many toasts show at once), `expand` and `id`.
2. **Viewport** (`ToastViewport`): a fixed list at the bottom centre of the screen, with 16px of padding; at most 448px wide from the `md` breakpoint.
3. **Toast** (`Toast`): the pill: `static-black` at 80% under a `static-white` 10% overlay, a 20px background blur ("Background blur 40"), a 16px radius, `static-white` text.
4. **Status icon** (optional, `status`): a success or error icon at the start, 16px (`sm`) or 20px (`lg`). The toast gets the status as `data-status`.
5. **Title** (`ToastTitle`) and **description** (`ToastDescription`, optional): regular text; the description in `static-white` at 80%.
6. **Action** (`action`, optional): an element after the text, e.g. an Undo button (`ToastAction`, a Filled button).
7. **Close button** (`ToastClose`, when `closable`): a 16px close icon in `static-white` at 80%, white on hover, with a 24px hit area.

`toast()` takes the props of `Toast` plus `title`, `description`, `action`, `status`, `size`, `closable`, `duration` and `toasterId`. It returns `{ id, dismiss, update }`. `useToast()` returns the current `toasts` (newest first; a closed one stays a few seconds with `open: false` while it animates out), `toast` and `dismiss(id?)`. The `reducer` export is deprecated and will be removed in 6.0: it is no longer what the store runs.

## Behaviour

- **Duration:** a toast closes after the Toaster's `duration` (3000ms); a `duration` passed to `toast()` wins. A toast with an `action` defaults to `Number.POSITIVE_INFINITY` instead of the Toaster's `duration`: it stays until the user closes it, unless you give it its own `duration`. `duration: Number.POSITIVE_INFINITY` keeps any toast until the user closes it.
- **How many:** a Toaster shows up to `limit` toasts at once (1 by default: a new `toast()` replaces the visible one). Over the limit, the oldest toast closes. A Toaster registers its `limit` before the page's own effects run, so toasts shown by a page as it mounts all show; a lazily loaded Toaster reopens the toasts closed before it mounted (while they are still in the store), up to its limit.
- **Stacking:** with a `limit` above 1 the toasts stack, as in Sonner: the newest in front, the next two each 8px higher and 5% smaller behind it, with their content hidden; older toasts wait hidden behind them. Hovering the stack or moving keyboard focus into it (F8, Tab) spreads it into a list, 8px apart, newest at the bottom; it collapses again once the pointer and focus have left (focus left by a click, on a toast's close button or action, doesn't hold it open). `expand` keeps it spread. A toast that closes behind another (over the limit, on its timer, dismissed) fades out in place. With reduced motion the toasts move at once and only fade. The front toast keeps the Figma look; the stack is the library's own.

<Canvas of={ToasterStories.Stacked} />

- **Pause:** hovering or focusing the toasts pauses their timers.
- **Dismiss:** Escape or a swipe (to the right; to the left in right-to-left text) closes a toast; so do `dismiss()` and its close button. `update()` changes a toast in place.
- **Several toasters:** every `<Toaster />` without an `id` shows the toasts that have no `toasterId`. Give a toaster an `id` and pass the same `toasterId` to `toast()` to run it separately; each keeps its own `limit`.

## States

- **Closable:** the Figma toast has no close button. Toasts with an `action` or an infinite `duration` get one by default; `closable` turns it on or off for any toast.

<Canvas of={ToasterStories.Closable} />

- **With an action:** an element after the text. The toast stays until it is dismissed (its `duration` defaults to `Number.POSITIVE_INFINITY`, and the Toaster's `duration` doesn't apply) and gets a close button by default, so the user has as long as they need to reach the action (WCAG 2.2.1). A `duration` passed to `toast()` closes it on a timer again (hovering or focusing it pauses the timer).

## Accessibility

- The viewport is a `region` landmark named "Notifications (F8)" (`messages.toast.region`, where `{hotkey}` becomes the shortcut).
- Each toast is announced in a live region when it appears, after "Notification" (`messages.toast.label`): assertively for the default `type="foreground"`, politely for `type="background"`.
- A toast is a focusable `<li>` in the viewport list. While focus or the pointer is in the region, the toasts don't close by themselves. Focus in a stack spreads it, so the toasts behind the front one are visible while you move through them.
- The close button is named "Close" (`messages.toast.close`); `aria-label` on `ToastClose` overrides it. It has the `focus-ring` and a 24px hit area (WCAG 2.5.8) around its 16px (`sm`) or 20px (`lg`) box.
- For an action, use `ToastAction` with `altText`: Radix requires it, and it tells screen-reader users how to do the action another way, since they hear the toast without moving to it (and a toast with its own `duration` may close before they reach it). `ToastAction` closes the toast when clicked.

### Keyboard

Radix Toast (no interaction test covers these keys):

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| F8 | move focus to the notifications region (`hotkey` on `ToastViewport`) |
| Tab / Shift + Tab | move through the toasts and the buttons in them; the region is in the tab order where `<Toaster />` (or `ToastViewport`) is rendered |
| Enter, Space | on a `ToastAction` or a `ToastClose`: run it and close the toast |
| Escape | close the focused toast. With focus elsewhere, close the newest toast, unless a layer opened after it (a dialog, a menu, a tooltip) takes Escape first |

### Deviations from the Figma kit

The full table is in the package README.

| Figma | Library | WCAG |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No close button: the toast closes itself after 3s | a toast with an `action` stays until it is dismissed (`duration` defaults to `Number.POSITIVE_INFINITY`); toasts with an `action` or an infinite `duration` get a close button by default (`closable`), so they can be dismissed with a pointer | 2.2.1 |
| "Focus" effect: 4px ring, black 4% (1.1:1) | the close button's `focus-ring`: the Figma ring plus a 2px `black-80` outline offset by 2px | 2.4.7, 1.4.11 |

## Localization and RTL

- Built-in strings: `messages.toast.close` ("Close", `aria-label` on `ToastClose`), `messages.toast.label` ("Notification", `label` on `ToastProvider`) and `messages.toast.region` (`Notifications ({hotkey})`, `label` on `ToastViewport`). `<Toaster />` renders them with the provider's messages.
- The viewport takes `dir` from `SnowUIProvider`. In right-to-left text the status icon is on the right, the close button on the left, and toasts are swiped away to the left.

## Theme

The viewport isn't portalled: toasts render inside `<Toaster />`, so they take the theme of wherever you place it. The toast itself uses static colours and looks the same in both themes; an action button follows the theme.

## Composition

`Toaster` covers most apps. To lay out toasts yourself, compose the parts, as the AllVariants story does:

```tsx
<ToastProvider duration={5000}>
  <Toast size="lg" duration={Number.POSITIVE_INFINITY} open={open} onOpenChange={setOpen}>
    <ToastTitle size="lg">Deleted</ToastTitle>
    <ToastAction altText="Restore it from the trash" onClick={undo}>
      Undo
    </ToastAction>
    <ToastClose size="lg" />
  </Toast>
  <ToastViewport />
</ToastProvider>
```

Pass the same `size` to `Toast`, `ToastTitle`, `ToastDescription`, `ToastAction` and `ToastClose`. The `Toaster` defaults don't apply to composed toasts: give a toast with an action `duration={Number.POSITIVE_INFINITY}` and a `ToastClose` yourself, as above.

## Do and don't

<DoDont>
<Do>

- Render one `<Toaster />` near the root of the app.
- Keep the title to a few words: "Saved", "Link copied".
- Use `status` for success and error, so the icon carries the meaning with the text.
- Give an action `ToastAction` with an `altText` that names another way to do it.

</Do>
<Dont>

- Don't put information the user must act on only in a toast.
- Don't show a toast for every small change: by default one message at a time is all the user sees, and a stack only shows the newest in full.
- Don't turn off the close button of a toast with an action or no timeout.
- Don't render a `<Toaster />` in every page component: they all show the same toasts.

</Dont>
</DoDont>

## Props

`Toaster` is this library's; the other parts are the [Radix Toast](https://www.radix-ui.com/primitives/docs/components/toast#api-reference) parts with the Figma styles. They take the props below, `className` (merged with the part's classes) and the attributes of the element they render.

### Toaster

Renders the toasts from `toast()` in a `ToastProvider` and a `ToastViewport`. `duration` (3000ms) applies to toasts without an `action` or a `duration` of their own; `id` shows only the toasts created with the same `toasterId`.

<ArgTypes of={Toaster} />

### ToastProvider

Holds the toasts of a hand-composed layout; it renders nothing. `duration` is 5000ms by default (Radix), `label` defaults to `messages.toast.label`, and `swipeDirection` to `right` (`left` in right-to-left text).

<ArgTypes of={ToastProvider} />

### ToastViewport

The `region` and the `<ol>` of toasts, fixed at the bottom centre. `label` defaults to `messages.toast.region`; `hotkey` (`["F8"]` by default) is the shortcut that focuses it.

<ArgTypes of={ToastViewport} />

### Toast

One toast, an `<li>`: `open` / `defaultOpen` / `onOpenChange`, its `duration`, `type` (`foreground` or `background`, for the announcement) and `size`. The status icon is drawn by `Toaster` only: a hand-composed `Toast` with `status` shows no icon.

<ArgTypes of={Toast} />

### ToastTitle

The title, a `<div>`; pass the toast's `size`.

<ArgTypes of={ToastTitle} />

### ToastDescription

The description, a `<div>` at 80% opacity; pass the toast's `size`.

<ArgTypes of={ToastDescription} />

### ToastAction

A Filled `<button>` that closes the toast when clicked. `altText` is required: the toast's announcement reads it in place of the button.

<ArgTypes of={ToastAction} />

### ToastClose

The close `<button>`, a 16px close icon named "Close" (`messages.toast.close`) unless you pass `aria-label`.
