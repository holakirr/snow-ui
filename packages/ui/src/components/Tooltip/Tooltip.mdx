Tooltip shows a short text hint about an element when the pointer rests on it or it gets keyboard focus: what an icon-only button does, its keyboard shortcut. It is the Figma "Tooltip" set (Dark and Light): a 24px-high label with a background blur. It is built on Radix Tooltip, so it needs a `TooltipProvider` above it.

```tsx
import { SearchIcon } from '@holakirr/snow-ui-icons'
import {
  Button,
  Tooltip,
  TooltipContent,
  TooltipProvider,
  TooltipShortcut,
  TooltipTrigger,
} from '@holakirr/snow-ui'

// Once, near the root of the app.
<TooltipProvider>
  <App />
</TooltipProvider>

<Tooltip>
  <TooltipTrigger asChild>
    <Button aria-label="Search" startContent={<SearchIcon />} />
  </TooltipTrigger>
  <TooltipContent>
    Search <TooltipShortcut>⌘K</TooltipShortcut>
  </TooltipContent>
</Tooltip>
```

## When to use

- The name or a short description of an icon-only button.
- A keyboard shortcut for a control.
- A few words that complete a truncated or abbreviated label.
- A short title and a sentence or two that explain a control or a value (a rich tooltip, added in 5.2).

## When not to use

- Information the user needs to complete a task: put it on the page, e.g. as a field description.
- Content with links, buttons or fields: [Popover](?path=/docs/components-popover--docs); a tooltip can't be interacted with by keyboard.
- A status message: a toast ([Toaster](?path=/docs/components-toaster--docs)).
- A hint on an element that can't take focus (plain text, a disabled button): keyboard users never see it.

## Anatomy

1. **Provider** (`TooltipProvider`): required above every `Tooltip` (Radix throws without one). It sets `delayDuration` (700ms before a tooltip opens) and `skipDelayDuration` (300ms in which the next tooltip opens at once) for the tooltips in it. `SnowUIProvider` doesn't include one; `SidebarProvider` has its own.
2. **Root** (`Tooltip`): the open state of one tooltip.
3. **Trigger** (`TooltipTrigger`): the element the tooltip describes, usually a Button (`asChild`).
4. **Content** (`TooltipContent`): the label, in a portal, 4px from the trigger (`sideOffset={4}`), above it by default (`side`). 24px high at least, 4 / 8px padding, a 12px radius, 12/16 text, a 10px background blur; items inside are 4px apart.
5. **Shortcut** (`TooltipShortcut`, optional): secondary text, e.g. a shortcut, at 70% opacity.
6. **Title** (`TooltipTitle`) and **description** (`TooltipDescription`), optional, added in 5.2: the kit's rich tooltip, a 12/16 semibold title over 12/16 text. With either inside, the content stacks them, start-aligned and 4px apart, with an 8px radius, and wraps the text at 280px. See [Rich tooltip](#rich-tooltip).

## Rich tooltip

Added in 5.2. The kit's multi-line tooltip ("This is a tooltip" over a few lines of text): put a `TooltipTitle` and a `TooltipDescription` in `TooltipContent`, or a `TooltipDescription` alone for a tip of two lines ("Compared with yesterday, it is up 11.01%"). The content sees them (`:has()`) and stacks them, start-aligned (on the right in right-to-left text) and 4px apart (the tooltip's gap); the radius becomes 8 and the width at most 280px, so the text wraps (both read from the kit's guidance frame, ≈8 and ≈280: the kit has no measured values for the rich tooltip; the padding, 12/16 text and colours stay the tooltip's). Both variants work; the colours stay the tooltip's.

```tsx
<TooltipContent>
  <TooltipTitle>This is a tooltip</TooltipTitle>
  <TooltipDescription>
    Tooltips are used to describe or identify an element.
  </TooltipDescription>
</TooltipContent>
```

<Canvas of={TooltipStories.Rich} />

- **Text only.** The kit's rich tooltip has no actions, and a tooltip can't hold any: it takes no focus and closes when the pointer or the focus leaves its trigger (WAI-ARIA Tooltip pattern). For a link or a button, use a [Popover](?path=/docs/components-popover--docs).
- **Read as one description:** the trigger's description is the title and the text ("This is a tooltip Tooltips are used…"); the parts are paragraphs, so a screen reader hears a space between them. Keep the title short.
- **Not for essential information**, as any tooltip: a touch screen has no hover.

## States

- **Closed** and **open:** controlled with `open` / `defaultOpen` / `onOpenChange` on `Tooltip`, or left to Radix: it opens after the provider's delay on hover, at once on keyboard focus.
- The tooltip has no hover, pressed or disabled state of its own.

## Portal and theme

`TooltipContent` renders in a portal at the end of `<body>`, outside your layout. So it takes the theme of `<html>`, not of a `data-theme` scope around the trigger. Set the theme on `<html>`, or give the tooltip its own scope: `<TooltipContent data-theme="dark">` (the prop is passed through).

## Accessibility

- The content is `role="tooltip"`. While it is open the trigger's `aria-describedby` points at it, so the text is the trigger's accessible description, not its name: an icon-only button still needs an `aria-label`. With `aria-label` on `TooltipContent`, screen readers get that text instead of the content.
- It opens on hover (after the provider's `delayDuration`) and on keyboard focus, but not when a pointer press focuses the trigger. The pointer can move from the trigger onto the tooltip without closing it.
- Blur, a click or a pointer press on the trigger close it.
- The tooltip is not modal and takes no focus: it can't hold interactive content.
- There are no built-in strings.

### Keyboard

Radix Tooltip, checked by the Default story's test; the trigger keeps its own keys:

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Tab, Shift + Tab | focusing the trigger shows the tooltip at once, without the hover delay; moving focus away hides it |
| Escape | hide the tooltip; focus stays on the trigger, and the next focus shows it again |
| Enter, Space | activate a button trigger; its click also hides the tooltip |

### Deviations from the Figma kit

The full table is in the package README.

| Figma | Library | WCAG |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Localization and RTL

- The tooltip renders no text of its own: its content is yours to translate.
- The portalled content takes `dir` from `SnowUIProvider`, since it is outside your layout's `dir` scope. Without a provider it has no `dir` and follows `<html dir>`.
- `side="left"` and `side="right"` are physical: they don't swap in right-to-left text.

## Composition (asChild)

`TooltipTrigger` takes `asChild` (Radix) to render your own element, which must be focusable (a Button, a link) so keyboard users get the tooltip too. The trigger's handlers compose with the tooltip's.

```tsx
<Tooltip>
  <TooltipTrigger asChild>
    <a href="/docs">Docs</a>
  </TooltipTrigger>
  <TooltipContent variant="light">Opens the documentation</TooltipContent>
</Tooltip>
```

## Do and don't

<DoDont>
<Do>

- Render one `TooltipProvider` near the root of the app.
- Keep the text to a few words (a title and a sentence or two in a rich tooltip), without links or buttons.
- Give an icon-only trigger its own `aria-label`; the tooltip only describes it.
- Put shortcuts in `TooltipShortcut`.

</Do>
<Dont>

- Don't put essential information only in a tooltip.
- Don't use a tooltip on an element that can't take focus.
- Don't put interactive content in a tooltip: use a Popover.
- Don't repeat the visible label of the trigger in its tooltip.

</Dont>
</DoDont>

## Props

`TooltipProvider`, `Tooltip`, `TooltipTrigger` and `TooltipContent` are the [Radix Tooltip](https://www.radix-ui.com/primitives/docs/components/tooltip#api-reference) parts with the Figma styles; `TooltipShortcut` is a `<span>`. They take the props below, `className` (merged with the part's classes) and the attributes of the element they render.

### TooltipProvider

Required above every `Tooltip`; it renders nothing. `delayDuration` (700ms by default) is the hover delay before a tooltip opens and `skipDelayDuration` (300ms) the time in which the next one opens without it. `disableHoverableContent` closes the tooltip as soon as the pointer leaves the trigger.

<ArgTypes of={TooltipProvider} />

### Tooltip

The root of one tooltip: it holds the open state and renders nothing. Controlled with `open` and `onOpenChange`, or uncontrolled with `defaultOpen`; `delayDuration` and `disableHoverableContent` override the provider's for this tooltip.

<ArgTypes of={Tooltip} />

### TooltipTrigger

A `<button>` described by the tooltip while it is open (`aria-describedby`); with `asChild` your focusable element (a Button, a link) is the trigger.

<ArgTypes of={TooltipTrigger} />

### TooltipContent

The `role="tooltip"` label, in a portal: `variant` (`dark` by default) picks the Figma variant. Here `sideOffset` is 4 by default; `side` is `top` (Radix), and it flips when there isn't room (`avoidCollisions`). `aria-label` replaces the text screen readers get.

<ArgTypes of={TooltipContent} />

### TooltipShortcut

A `<span>` for secondary text, e.g. a shortcut, at 70% opacity.

<ArgTypes of={TooltipShortcut} />

### TooltipTitle

Added in 5.2. A `<p>` in 12/16 semibold, the first line of a rich tooltip. See [Rich tooltip](#rich-tooltip).

<ArgTypes of={TooltipTitle} />

### TooltipDescription

Added in 5.2. A `<p>` for the text of a rich tooltip, under its title or alone; it wraps at 280px. See [Rich tooltip](#rich-tooltip).
