Tabs switch between panels of content, one shown at a time. They are built on the Radix Tabs primitive and styled as the Figma Tab set: `TabsList variant` picks Underline (`line`, the default), Pill, Icon-toggle or Solid, and `size` picks one of three sizes. The segmented variants share their items with [Toggle](?path=/docs/components-input-toggle--docs) and [ToggleGroup](?path=/docs/components-input-togglegroup--docs).

```tsx
import { Tabs, TabsContent, TabsList, TabsTrigger } from '@holakirr/snow-ui'

<Tabs defaultValue="account">
  <TabsList>
    <TabsTrigger value="account">Account</TabsTrigger>
    <TabsTrigger value="password">Password</TabsTrigger>
  </TabsList>
  <TabsContent value="account">…</TabsContent>
  <TabsContent value="password">…</TabsContent>
</Tabs>
```

<Primary />

## When to use

- Related content split into sections at the same level, where the user needs one section at a time: settings pages, a card with several views.
- The underline tabs for page sections; the segmented variants for compact switchers inside a card or a toolbar.

## When not to use

- A view or mode switcher without panels (a chart's day / week / month): [ToggleGroup](?path=/docs/components-input-togglegroup--docs). Every tab points at its panel, so a tab list without panels is invalid ARIA.
- Site navigation: a [Sidebar](?path=/docs/components-sidebar--docs) or a [Breadcrumb](?path=/docs/components-breadcrumb--docs). (Tabs whose panels are routes can still render links, see Composition.)
- Content the user compares or reads in sequence: show it on one page, or use an [Accordion](?path=/docs/components-accordion--docs).

## Anatomy

1. **Root** (`Tabs`): the Radix Tabs root. It holds the value and has no styles of its own.
2. **List** (`TabsList`): the `tablist`. `variant` and `size` pass to its triggers. For `pill` and `icon-toggle` it is the Black/4% track.
3. **Trigger** (`TabsTrigger`): a `tab` with a `value`. `icon` renders an icon before the label; an icon without children makes an icon-only tab. The `line` variant adds the 2px underline below the label.
4. **Panel** (`TabsContent`): the `tabpanel` of the same `value`, 8px below the list, with a 4px radius for its focus ring.

## Variants

| `variant` | Figma | Look | Use for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `line` (default) | Underline | text tabs 16px apart; the active one `primary` with a 2px Primary line | page and card sections |
| `pill` | Pill | Button items on a Black/4% track with a 10px background blur; the active item White/80% with the `shadow-2` shadow | compact switchers |
| `icon-toggle` | Icon-toggle | the pill track; inactive items show only their icon, the active one its icon and label | switchers in a tight space (give every trigger an `icon`) |
| `solid` | Solid | no track; the active item has a Black/4% fill | switchers on a busy surface |

## Sizes

`TabsList size` sets the size of every trigger. The `line` tabs change their text style; the segmented items take the Figma Button sizes.

| `size` | `line` text | `line` icon | Item min height | Item padding | Item radius | Item text | Icon-only padding / glyph | Track padding / radius |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `sm` | 12/16 | 12px | 24px | 4 / 12px | 12px | 12/16 | 4px / 16px | 2px / 16px |
| `md` (default) | 14/20 | 16px | 36px | 8 / 16px | 16px | 14/20 | 8px / 20px | 4px / 20px |
| `lg` | 16/24 | 20px | 48px | 12 / 20px | 20px | 16/24 | 12px / 24px | 4px / 24px |

Segmented items are 2px (`sm`) or 4px apart. Inactive `icon-toggle` items are squares of the item's min height. Icons are sized unless they have a `size-*` class.

## States

- **Inactive:** `text-secondary` label and icon.
- **Hover and keyboard focus:** the label turns black; keyboard focus adds the `focus-ring` utility.
- **Active** (`data-state="active"`): `line` tabs get a `primary` label and the Primary underline; segmented items a black label and the variant's fill.
- **Disabled:** `disabled` on a trigger gives it a Black/20% label and a not-allowed cursor (segmented items also a Black/4% tile). The keyboard skips it.

The colour is a custom property (`--tab-fg` for `line`, `--segment-fg` for the segmented variants) that the states change. A `text-*` class sets the colour in every state; `[--tab-fg:…]` or `[--segment-fg:…]` changes only the rest colour.

## Accessibility

- Radix renders a `tablist` of `tab`s, each with `aria-selected` and `aria-controls` pointing at its `tabpanel`. Render a `TabsContent` for every trigger.
- An icon-only tab needs an `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`; without one, `TabsTrigger` logs a development warning. In `icon-toggle`, the labels of inactive tabs are visually hidden, not removed, so every tab keeps its name.
- Several tab lists on a page need distinct names: pass `aria-label` to `TabsList`.

### Keyboard

The tab list is one tab stop (roving focus, Radix Tabs):

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Tab | move into the list, onto the selected tab; press again to move to the panel, which is focusable |
| ArrowRight / ArrowLeft | move to the next / previous tab and select it (in a `vertical` list: ArrowDown / ArrowUp); disabled tabs are skipped and focus wraps around. In right-to-left text ArrowLeft moves to the next tab |
| Home / End | move to the first / last tab and select it |
| Enter, Space | select the focused tab (with `activationMode="manual"`, where the arrow keys only move focus) |

### Deviations from the Figma kit

The full table is in the package README.

| Figma | Library | WCAG |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Focus" effect: a 4px Black/4% ring (1.1:1); no focus state on Tab | `focus-ring`: the Figma ring plus a 2px `black-80` outline offset by 2px (12.6:1 light, 8.7:1 dark) | 2.4.7, 1.4.11 |
| Inactive tabs (every variant) at 40% layer opacity: the label is 2.85:1 | the label and icon are `text-secondary` (5.74:1 light, 7.08:1 dark), black on hover and keyboard focus (`primary` for the active Underline tab); disabled tabs keep Black/20% | 1.4.3, 1.4.11 |
| Icon-only tabs: — | a development warning without `aria-label` / `aria-labelledby` | 4.1.2 |

## Localization and RTL

- Tabs render no built-in strings: the labels are yours.
- The list follows the text direction: in right-to-left text the first tab is on the right. The arrow keys follow the `dir` of `SnowUIProvider`: ArrowLeft moves to the next tab.

## Composition (asChild)

`TabsTrigger asChild` makes the only child, e.g. a router link, the tab. The trigger's `icon`, label wrapper and underline are rendered inside it, around its content. The state colours don't depend on `:enabled`, so a link gets them in every state.

```tsx
<TabsList aria-label="Sections">
  <TabsTrigger value="overview" asChild>
    <RouterLink to="/overview">Overview</RouterLink>
  </TabsTrigger>
</TabsList>
```

<Canvas of={TabsStories.Links} />

The selected tab is `value` (controlled) or `defaultValue` (uncontrolled), with `onValueChange(value)`, on `Tabs`.

## Do and don't

<DoDont>
<Do>

- Render a panel for every tab.
- Keep tab labels short, one or two words.
- Give icon-only tabs an `aria-label`, and every `icon-toggle` trigger an `icon`.
- Name each tab list when a page has several.

</Do>
<Dont>

- Don't use tabs without panels to switch a view: use a ToggleGroup.
- Don't use tabs for steps the user must complete in order.
- Don't mix icon-only and labelled tabs in one list.
- Don't put `text-*` on a trigger when you only want a different rest colour: set `[--tab-fg:…]` or `[--segment-fg:…]`.

</Dont>
</DoDont>

## Props

The parts are the [Radix Tabs](https://www.radix-ui.com/primitives/docs/components/tabs#api-reference) parts with the Figma styles: they take the Radix props below, `className` (merged with the part's classes) and the attributes of the element they render.

### Tabs

The root, a `<div>`: which tab is selected (controlled with `value` and `onValueChange`, or uncontrolled with `defaultValue`), the orientation (`horizontal` by default) and how the keyboard selects (`activationMode`, `automatic` by default).
