TextStrip is a pill-shaped line of text, 160×28: a label that stands out as a block, such as the name of a plan, a step or a section. It is the Figma "TextStrip": Black/4% with semibold text, or, with `strip`, Secondary/Indigo to highlight one of several.

```tsx
import { TextStrip } from '@holakirr/snow-ui'

<TextStrip>Starter</TextStrip>
<TextStrip strip>Pro</TextStrip>
```

<Primary />

## When to use

- A short label shown as a block of equal width, often in a column: plans, steps, categories.
- Highlighting the current one of those blocks with `strip`.

## When not to use

- A status or a category inside a line of data: use [Chip](?path=/docs/components-chip--docs).
- Switching between views: use [ToggleGroup](?path=/docs/components-input-togglegroup--docs) or [Tabs](?path=/docs/components-tabs--docs), which manage the selection and the keyboard.
- A segmented progress bar: use [Strip](?path=/docs/components-strip--docs).

## Anatomy

A single `<div>` (or the `asChild` element): 160×28 (`w-40 h-7`), fully rounded, 12px of padding on each side, with its text centred on one 14px line and cut with an ellipsis when it doesn't fit.

## States

`strip` sets `data-state="on"` (otherwise `"off"`) for your own styles. TextStrip has no hover or focus state of its own: with `asChild` on a button or a link, style those on your element (the AsButtons story adds `focus-ring`).

## Accessibility

- TextStrip has no role: its text is read in the flow of the page. When `strip` marks the current or the selected item, say so in the markup too — `aria-pressed` on a toggle button, `aria-current` on a link or a step — since the colour alone isn't read out (WCAG 1.3.1, 1.4.1).
- **Contrast:** Figma's white text on Secondary/Indigo is 2.07:1; the text is static black (10.15:1), as on the Badge (1.4.3). The pills themselves are close to the page (Black/4% is 1.1:1, the indigo 2.07:1 on white): with more contrast, the Black/4% pill gets the `control-border` stroke and the strip a 2px `black-80` ring, so the highlighted one differs by more than its colour (1.4.11).

## Localization and RTL

- TextStrip renders no built-in strings.
- The text is centred in both directions; in right-to-left text the ellipsis is on the left.

## Composition (asChild)

`asChild` renders the only child with the strip's classes and `data-state`, merged with the child's own classes (which win conflicts): a `<button>` in a group of choices, a link, a heading.

## Do and don't

<DoDont>
<Do>

- Keep the text short enough to fit 160px, or widen the strip.
- Mark the strip in the markup (`aria-pressed`, `aria-current`) when it means "selected" or "current".

</Do>
<Dont>

- Don't use TextStrip for long text: it is cut to one line.
- Don't build a tab bar from TextStrips: use Tabs.

</Dont>
</DoDont>
